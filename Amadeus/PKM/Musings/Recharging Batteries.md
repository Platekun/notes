# Recharging Batteries

It’s crazy to think how fast our holy week has gone by. I can remember how I worked a lot in just two days. Besides work I went to dinner with Lourdes to celebrate her birthday, about 25 people came including her family. That fact alone kept me in the edge as I become nervous because of big crowds.

The next day Lourdes insisted on coming to my apartment and since buying food was not an option, I decided to cook for her entire family by myself. Then from the afternoon to the night, I spent the entire with her making sure she was accompanied during her birthday.

With my batteries drained, I spent today by myself alone. I did some laundry chores, organized my office closet and even installed led lights on the desk and bed.

Let’s see how much energy she has for Saturday but I think she doesn’t get tired while I’m really quick to burn out when surrounded by people.
