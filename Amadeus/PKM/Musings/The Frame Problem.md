# The Frame Problem

> *[[Hiyajo Maho]]: An AI doesn't know that. You need to tell it exactly what a box is.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: For this experiment you assumed that Robo-Kiryu had a basic idea of what a box was.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: But as you've just seen, adjust the information they receive even a little, and an AI won't do what you want it to.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Also there's only this one box on the table, but if there were a square stone that looked just like a box, you'd need an order to differentiate the two.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: If the stop was on top of the box, you'd first need to give it an order to move it, too.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: So you need to remove each possibility, one by one.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: You'd never be done, huh?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Yup. Never.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: You don't know what conditions can affect the AI's judgement, so you need to include every possibility you can think of.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Kiryu, what did you have for breakfast today?

[[Kiryu Moeka]]: … For breakfast doay?

What was this about?

[[Kiryu Moeka]]: … A jam bun.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Did you have any choices beside jam buns for breakfast?

[[Kiryu Moeka]]:… A meat bun.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: If you'd chosen the meat bun, your actions might've changed. You need to take into account, too.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Also… Hmm, there's the weather, as well, It's clear out today, but if it was raining, you might have to take that into account.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: There's also the temperature and humidity of the room. The color of the table's important as well. Also you'd need to measure its height from the floor and know what type of material it's made out of.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: That curtain's open, but what if it was closed?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: You'd also need to test the cases where it was half-open and 75% open individually as well.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: The cleanliness of the curtain might play a factor, too.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: When's the last time it was cleaned? You also need to consider how much you paid, and the cleaner's attitude, as parameters.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: And…

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Stop, stop.

I had to stop her before she went any further.

[[Okabe Rintaro]] : There's no way this morning's breakfast could influence whether or not she picks a box up off a table.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Let alone how clean the curtain is.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: So couldn't you just take them off the list?

Maho's eyes began to shine.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: That's it! That's exactly right!

[[Okabe Rintaro]] : Huh?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: You're exactly right, Okabe.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Breakfast and the weather have nothing to do with opening a box.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Then..

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: But how did you know that?

Huh?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Well I mean, normally…

And then finally, I understood the point of this experiment. Maho nodded, satisfied.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: That's right. An AI doesn't know what “normal” is.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: … I see.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: That's the frame problem.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: An AI needs to consider an endless number of attached conditions to know how to execute an order.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: The vast majority of those conditions can probably be ignored, but the AI doesn't

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: The vast majority of those conditions can probably be ignored, but the AI doesn't know which to ignore.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Which means it has to consider everything.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: But “everything” is an infinite set, isn't it?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: If it has to examine an infinite number of conditions, it will never be able to pick up that box.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: A human knows without having to think that breakfast and table's color have nothing to do with opening a box.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: But an AI doesn't.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: A human can easily frame which conditions need to be accounted for, but an AI can't.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: So that's the frame problem.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: I see, interesting.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Isn't it?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: But why are humans able to create a frame?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: We have no idea. It's been one of the mysteries of the AI field for 60 years.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: One of the goals of the Amadeus project is solving the frame problem.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: When you look at [Kurisu], it may feel she's solved it.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: But we don't know how.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: In fact, neither does she.*
