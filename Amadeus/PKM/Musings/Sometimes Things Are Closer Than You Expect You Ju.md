# Sometimes Things Are Closer Than You Expect You Just Need To Reach For Them

The recursive mother goose ending for Kagarii (and For Mayurii) showed us how sometimes we can be lost in our lives. The gut punch came with the meaning of the song Mayurii taught Kagarii which implies the song is for people always remember they are not alone and if the treasure they lost (love) is near them, they only need to reach for it.
