# Selling Refactors

Refactoring more always than not should be done for the sake of increasing shipping speed in terms of code understandability. Refactoring just for the sake of refactoring won’t be useful to the business and it is difficult to justify in front of others.

This is why a problem has to be found so we are allowed to refactor.

> *If you’re not refactoring, yes.
If you’re not refactoring with intent of delivering value, yes.
Remember bad code is only bad when it does not help you deliver your backlog at a sustainable*
