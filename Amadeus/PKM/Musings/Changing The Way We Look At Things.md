# Changing The Way We Look At Things

I believe what I can take from this is sometimes we already have the solution in plain view, it's hiding there however it requires a [[A Detective's Iron Rule|different angle]] to reach it. Maybe this is also a way of understanding when you're happy with life current situation.

> *[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: Find the sword.

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: You have to find it.

She always said the same thing whenever we met.

There was a chance Ayase was on my side, but having her say the same damn thing every time was getting on my nerves.

I wanted to find it, too. I just didn’t know how.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: I-I need a hint.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: H-How did you get it?

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: One day, I just had it.

That again? Was she just screwing with me?

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: I didn’t notice at first.

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: But I changed the way I looked at things, and I understood.

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: Maybe you can already see it.

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: You just don’t.*
