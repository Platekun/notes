# The Loneliness of Strength: Reflections on Satoru Gojo and Personal Growt

Monday, again (again? again). I'm starting this week after a long week of refactoring and a weekend I spent with Lourdes. I went to the movies on Saturday despite my headache and on Sunday I shared quality time with my family and my dad who was recently operated.

Lately I have been interested in the character of Satoru Gojo, a character form a series named Jujutsu Kaisen which is called “The strongest”. I mentioned this topic to Lourdes as well, I told her how being “The strongest” brought loneliness along with it since you are not able to connect with your peers. “Are you the strongest because of who you are? or are you who you are because you are the strongest” This quote from Suguru Geto somewhat reminded it of what I lived through my childhood and how I stand above many people right now. There is an interesting relationship between the mindset of being poo, being strong and taking responsibility for our actions.
