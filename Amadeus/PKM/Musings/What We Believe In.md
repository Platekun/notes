# What We Believe In

I feel that this is Arimura's way of accepting that she needs something to believe in, despite that being a delussion. We, humans need something to believe in in order to navigate through life. In character arcs this is called "the lie the character believes” or "the truth the character believes”

> *[[Hinae Arimura]]: No… It really is my fault. My heart was weak. That’s why…

[[Takuru Miyashiro]] : Arimura…

[[Hinae Arimura]]: But I realized something. You can’t divide the world up into truth and lies.

[[Hinae Arimura]]: The world is half truth and half lies, and we have to accept that.

[[Hinae Arimura]]: Otherwise, you just can’t keep going.*
>
