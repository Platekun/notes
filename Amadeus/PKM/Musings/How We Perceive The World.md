# How We Perceive The World

It's interesting to not how [[Aoi Sena]] says in the same tone. In this piece, I really like the fact that it establishes that everybody has negative thoughts. The problem are not the negative thoughts but rather how we chose to face them. That's where a [[A Will That Can Turn Negatives Into Positives|strong will]] enters the picture.

As a side note, this piece also reinforces the idea of the Di-Sword as our reflection of our inner self / character.
