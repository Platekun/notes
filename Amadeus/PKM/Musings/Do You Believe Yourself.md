# Do You Believe Yourself?

Wednesday passed, had to give up on hanging out at Karen's birthday (Lourdes’ ex-boss). I hate myself for not stepping up as her boyfriend when needed but I'm in a tight situation with work right now.

According to my manager, I cannot go back to Walmart and Thresholds is only going to last up to Feb 9, which means I will be going to bench after that date. If we add the economic context we are in, I guess this could not look any better: A overworked employee which will end up in bench and probably fired if not project comes around.

From what It seems, they are already trying to find a project for me. This time looks like I can't say no since I don't have anything back. I talked about this with Lourdes, it reminded me of [[Escapism through Acceptance]].

If the worst case comes to happen, do I believe in myself to find a job? I am starting to see the bad luck smiling at me.
