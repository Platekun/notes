# Don't Let Peaceful Times Blind You From An Incoming Future

> *[[Suzuha Amane]]: Lately… I'm starting to not understand myself anymore.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: This… This may be a weird thing to think, but…

[[Suzuha Amane]]: Is it really okay to erase this world?

Her mind flashed back to young Kagari's screams.

- You can't erase this world, huh?

She'd never forgotten the look in Kagari's eyes and she'd never been able to shake the feeling that they were watching her, somehow.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: If my mission succeeds. Kurisu Maikuse won't die, and the door to the Steins Gate will open.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: Yeah.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: And that means that this world… you, mom, Mayu-nee-san and Rumi-nee-san and Okari-ojii-san…none of this will have ever happened.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: Yeah.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: The me that's here right now will never have met you…

[[Itaru Hashida]]: Hmm? Is this the scene where you confess your love? Should I save my game?

[[Suzuha Amane]]: …

Suzuha said nothing, and poked her dad's calf with her toes.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: I'm being serious. One more joke and I'll kick you.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: You're not sure what to do?

[[Suzuha Amane]]: …

[[Itaru Hashida]]: Well, um… you know, is it really that big of a deal?

[[Suzuha Amane]]: Huh?

[[Itaru Hashida]]: Who cares if this present disappears?

[[Itaru Hashida]]: I mean, a lot of people are going to die after this, right? I don't want that to happen.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: …

[[Itaru Hashida]]: You're overthinking things. You don't want a war, so you're going to change the world. Isn't it better when it's simple like that?*
