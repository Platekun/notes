# Top-down Memory Search Signals

Science understands the brain has a specific way of working, signals travels in different ways throughout the nervous system. In software development, data flow is not often considered by developers and the responsibility may fall on the senior / architect of the team. It's curious how Okabe speaks about the way top-down memory signals work as it is from a specification however there isn't one for applications.

The proliferation of different architectures, the network chasms, and many other factors has surely creeped into this topic and maybe it is why we cannot have a standard way of writing apps.
