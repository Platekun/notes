# I Am The Master Of My Fate

I have come to notice that people always blame their circumstances to “*fate*” (This Fate also comes in the form of a deity sometimes called “*God*” or even “*Time*”).  These people are the ones who when questioned about their decisions, they show a lack of care for their own lives. Those people never attempt to take course of their own lives, they never attempt to go higher and improve their quality of life. They instead just blame their shortcomings to their “*fate*”.

What even is fate? In this life, I have come to see there are things that are entirely outside of our control, this is the book definition of “*fate*”. A hard concept to grasp but one that does not makes me less responsible of my decisions because “***I am the master of my fate***”.

I will use anecdotic experience to explain my reasoning, in this last year (2021-2022):

- I became unemployed because I made wrong decisions when joining a company. There were things outside of my control inside the company but I was the one who made the desicion of moving there.
- I did not return to work right away after parting with the company but I was the one who made the decision of taking a break for 6 months before taking another job.
- I returned to my previous because I needed money. There were things outside of my control inside the company (I burnt out because of my client) but I was the one who made the decision to return.

I can’t control everything, but I have a say in my own life. There are things happening all the time, we can’t help that but we are free to decide, just like I did.

If you are getting what I am saying here you will see we have free will regarding our lives, maybe what we should be asking ourselves is [[The Ultimate Goal Of A Right-Sider|how to make good decisions]]?
