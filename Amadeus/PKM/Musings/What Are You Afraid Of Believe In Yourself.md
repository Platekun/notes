# What Are You Afraid Of? Believe In Yourself

Feels like I was interested in the way [[Okabe Rintaro]] phrased this. He is trying to come up with courage in order to save his loved one. He resorts to making logical statements in order to back up his claims. This is somewhat similar to a war cry. I think I usually have those moments where I can only think in “*Believe in yourself*”.

> *Will I be able to handle the enraged Doctor Nakabachi?

One mistake, and Kurisu wil die.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: What are you afraid of, Hououin Kyoma?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Everything's going according to plan.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Operation Skuld is flawless. You spent fifteen years perfecting it, remember?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Believe in yourself. Believe in Hououin Kyoma.
