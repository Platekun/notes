# What Is CloudMap?

The CloudMap service is a service provided by [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is AWS|AWS]] used to perform [service discovery](https://app.notion.com/p/9a21ca0d59e24545bbfc29a4c8e07673?pvs=21) with our AWS resources.
