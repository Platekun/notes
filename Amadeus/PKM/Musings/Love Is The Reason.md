# Love Is The Reason

I usually get this feeling that we are out of topics to talk about. This makes me think that we do not have much things in common. Despite that, this thought always comes back and reminds me why we are together.

> *[[Koyomi Araragi]]*: *Sometimes, I don’t now why I’m dating you.

[[Koyomi Araragi]]*: *But I love you so much that I don’t need a reason.*
>
