# Being Serious Is Not A Virtue

I think I can empathize with Shinjo. I always try to have a professional attitude and can end up being too serious about things. This leads to stress and burnouts in some cases.

[[Ban Yasuji]]  reminds me of one of my leaders, Fred. We clashed sometimes but I learned to appreciate his easygoing attitude with problems.

Nowadays I feel I want to be a little bit more like him when I grow up. It's okay to have fun and relax sometimes. I guess it depends on the situation.

> [[Katsuko Momose]] *: You know why you are so exhausted? It’s cause you are taking this so seriously. You should relax a bit, like [[Ban Yasuji|he]] did.

[[Takeshi Shinjo]] : I have no intention of copying [[Ban Yasuji|his]] habits.

[[Katsuko Momose]] : You’re so serious, aren’t you? If you keep on thinking that’s a virtue, you are going to have a bad time of it one day.*
