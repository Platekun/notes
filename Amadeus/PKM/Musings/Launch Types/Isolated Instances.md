# Isolated Instances

- **Dedicated Hosts**
    - Model: Rent entire physical servers to manage and control our instances.
    - Pros: Full visibility of the hardware used.
    - Use case: Needing to meet corporate compliance requirements.
- **Dedicated Instances**
    - Model: Rent [[What is EC2|EC2]] instances that are guaranteed to not share hardware with other customers.
    - Cons: Does not offer visibility of the hardware used.
