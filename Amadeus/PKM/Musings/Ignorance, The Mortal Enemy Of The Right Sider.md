# Ignorance, The Mortal Enemy Of The Right Sider

> *The patients enjoy the thrilling murder as they don’t connect the horrors to their personal lives allowing the persecution to continue blindly. The contradiction in taking enjoyment from the suffering of others is something Takuru’s only able to realize when he is able to relate to the victims through being so close to them. The downward thinking mindset that gives solace  to those unfortunate by giving them someone lesser is something that feeds ignorance. The mortal enemy of the right sider.*
