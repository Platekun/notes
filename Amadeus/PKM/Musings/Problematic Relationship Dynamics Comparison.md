# Problematic Relationship Dynamics Comparison

This extract reminded me of the dynamics of the relationship between Okabe, Kurisu and Mayuri and Lourdes, Nancy and Me. I once told Lourdes I associated her with Mayuri because she is his north star just like she's mine. I can be spouting nonsense about tech stuff and crazy experiments but she always bring me back to the real world.

I didn't notice that by making that comparison, I left out that Okabe is actually in love with Kurisu and I'd hate to extrapolate to say this was Nancy. I think this extract hit me in the gut because just like the extract implies, Lourdes also said we were a perfect match because of our common interest.
