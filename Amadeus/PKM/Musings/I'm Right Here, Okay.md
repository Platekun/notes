# I'm Right Here, Okay?

I captured this because this scene reminded me about the times loneliness attacked me while I was alone. Lourdes always said something similar to what Rimi said here. I fear for the future where I will be alone, and while I know loneliness by itself is not ideal, a bad company is even worse than being alone. I'm speaking about my lack of friends and people who I can rely on. This is why I can only try to be strong at the face of anything. Am unbreakable wall.

> *[[Takumi Nishijou]]: No…No…

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Help me! Somebody help me!

I screamed.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: My head felt like it was tearing itself apart. I was going crazy.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Aaah! Waaah!

I kept looking and looking and looking.

She wasn’t here. She wasn’t here. She wasn’t here. Why wasn’t she here?

She’d left to go to the store.

I fell to the ground, head in my hands.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Help me…Rimi… Please, help me! Don’t leave me…Please

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: I won’t

There was an answer.

I gasped and looked up, and Rimi was standing right next to me, holding a bag from the store.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Rimi…

She was back… Whew, Relieved I stretched out a hand.

Rimi crouched down and took it with both hands.

The last time I’d touched them, Rimi’s hands were cold. but now they were so warm.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: I’m here

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: I’m right here, okay?

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: Don’t be afraid.

Rimi smiled gently at me and wiped my tears away with her fingers. I was really pathetic.

Crying, screaming, being afraid like this in front of a girl…

But Rimi said she’d be with me anyway.

Right now, the only thing keeping my mind from shattering was this warmth.

I didn’t want to let go of this hand, not until I had the Di-sword.*
