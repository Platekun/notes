# After A Short Vacation

This weekend that just passed, I spent it by myself. Lourdes needed to travel to Valledupar to visit a part of the family she has there which leaft me with lots of solitude and free time.

It feels kind of weird, I really liked the ability to make use of my time as I wanted, nobody made me went out when I didn't want and instead I used my time enjoying one of my hobbies. Reading the novel however showed me that even the main character needed somebody when he was at his lowest, in his case he cried for Rimi Sakihata and when she disappeared everything around him went downhilll and didn't had anybody to lean on. It was so pathetic (in his words) that he reached the point where we was going to kill some bulllies before being stopped.

I have gave it some thought and I feel that I really need Lourdes in my life, she complements some aspects that without her I could end up like [[Takumi Nishijou]]. Not sure if that is 100% possible but my instinct tells me It could happen. Spending my days in work, then on weekends just doing chores and playing videogames/novels. No time for social interaction…For some reason sounds kinda sad, despite all those being things I like. I think the point I'm trying to make is that she keeps me at bay from going too far this way.

I wonder how mealprepping would work in my schedule?
