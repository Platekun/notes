# I Am The Captain Of My Soul

[Robotics;Notes Opening HD.mp4](I%20Am%20The%20Captain%20Of%20My%20Soul/RoboticsNotes_Opening_HD.mp4)

[Robotics;Notes Elite OP - The Promised Augment - English Subbed.mp4](I%20Am%20The%20Captain%20Of%20My%20Soul/RoboticsNotes_Elite_OP_-_The_Promised_Augment_-_English_Subbed.mp4)

Don’t act like most people do, living like side characters to their own lives. That’s what makes most people boring (and bored).

Instead live your life as if you were a protagonist. That doesn’t mean the world works around you, that is just plain egocentrism. I am talking about being the protagonist of **your own story**.

Just like protagonists, you will be put you through different “*arcs*” on your life, some will be positive and some will be negative to your character. It is important to remember that negative arcs do not last forever and when we face them we are entirely responsible for solving our problems.

It is also our responsibility not to become a side character in our story. Protagonists have dreams and act towards them.
