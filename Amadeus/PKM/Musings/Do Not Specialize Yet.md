# Do Not Specialize Yet

Always make sure to explore different things before specializing in something. You can miss out a lot of you decide to filter from knowing new things just for the sake of specialization.

> *I want to be clear: CSS is incredibly valuable and it should be valued
But that doesn't mean it's good *career advice* to specialize in it (or anything) before you've explored a bit and you know you can build a career in it
Just because it *should* be valued doesn't mean it is*
