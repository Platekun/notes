# Feelings Over Facts

This is the most difficult thing when trying to come up with the [[The Ultimate Goal Of A Right-Sider|right desicion]]. Feelings can make a mess in our heads and cloud us from the right course of action. In this case, even if she is the killer it would make more sense for [[Takuru Miyashiro]]  to gather more information before doing anything.

> *[[Serika Onoe]]*: Don't worry. I won't put you in danger. In fact, it would help me a lot if you stayed safe.

[[Serika Onoe]]: But you need to hear about the Committee. and the case. You'll need it to protect yourself. And-

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Stop it!

I screamed. I couldn't take anymore.

Stop it, Serika!

Stop talking like you're on my side!

You were the one who killed Yui and Kurusu, and you were the one who did that to Itou!

[[Serika Onoe]]:You're prioritizing your feelings over the facts? Is that what a right-sider is supposed to do?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Shut up.

I know that!

But what If I can't help it?

I can't just calm down and listen to you.
