# Present vs Future

Robotic;Notes’ protagonist mentioned this while arguing regarding some decisions regarding their robot project.

Subaru is usually a direct type of character and makes this remark on Akiho’s attitude. This stings for some reason. I think that depends on my mood at the moment.

Focusing on the future can guide our current actions, but focusing only on the present can also lead to poor plannification. I cannot seem to grasp a good balance between looking at the future and looking at the present.

If my present is dark, I look for a brighter future. If my future looks dark, I try to think of the present and enjoying the moment. Is this what life is about? I wonder.

> *[[Subaru Hidaka]]*: *She’s only looking at the now, not the future. That seems like nonsense to me.*
