# Warrior Mindset In Peaceful Times

> *The exhaustion was starting to take its toll on her.

During the war, she'd been able to keep herself sharp at all times, and this level of exhaustion wouldn't have meant anything.

The relaxed atmosphere of 2010 was actually making her realize how tired she was.

She was feeling very sleepy right now, actually.

She was surprised at how willing she was to fall asleep in such an undefended position, but she didn't think she could beat the temptation.*
>
