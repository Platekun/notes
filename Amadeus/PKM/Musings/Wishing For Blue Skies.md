# Wishing For Blue Skies

Alright I think this is linked to what she says later in game about how the sky [[Reality Synchro|looks kind of gloomy]]. The “sky” is the main topic in this piece, and finding that “*sky*” is simple as “*wishing for it”*. Is this talking about happiness? Beats me. What at least I can understand is that we can shape the form of our mental world despite our [[How We Perceive The World|negative feelings]].

> *[[Rimi Sakihata]]: What is that sky…

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: So blue, like it makes you cry?

— Because that's what I wished for.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: I didn't wish for it.

— I did.

— This is your mental world, but it's mine too.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: Hey...

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: Where can I find that sky?

Anywhere, if you wish for it.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: Is it okay...for me to wish?*
