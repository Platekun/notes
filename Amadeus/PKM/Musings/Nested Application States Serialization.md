# Nested Application States Serialization

Navigation libraries or routers do not often take into account super-deep nested UI states. Should it be taken into account? It does not seem possible.

This seems related to [[Application States Cannot Be Entirely Serialized]] .

> *I feel native navigation libs don't take into account nested structures, Like a Tab nested inside a screen or may be like a screen having Top and bottom tabs which is quite common in few mobile apps. Though they provide support they don't properly integrate it into the app.*
