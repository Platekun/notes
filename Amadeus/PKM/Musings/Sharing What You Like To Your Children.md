# Sharing What You Like To Your Children

In Robotic;Notes I found this moment, Misaki and her father where discussing about her likes to robots and it seems it originated from the father’s desire to share what he liked. The act of sharing this shaped lots of the behavior Misa and her sister took.

Sharing likes with your children can influence a lot in their futures. I should be careful about this.

> *[[Misaki Senomiya]]: What’s that supposed to mean? You know this is your fault, right?

[[Kenichiro Senomiya]]: How so?

[[Misaki Senomiya]]: Don’t tell me you were unaware of it all this time.

[[Misaki Senomiya]]: Ever since Aki and I were little, you’d go on and on 
about how amazing robot anime was.
