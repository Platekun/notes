# Deceiving Yourself To Avoid Reality: A Painful Awakening Call

This is the awakening call for Okabe. This is the moment he realizes he didn't save anyone nor himself. Okabe ended up deceiving himself to avoid reality instead of deceiving the world to make his reality be true.

If the lesson from [[The Illusion That Time Has Stopped]] is that averting our eyes from reality and looking for escapism is a common symptom of depression. Then from this we can follow up by stating that this wake-up call the logical conclusion of what happens when we take too long to wake up.

> *[[Itaru Hashida]]: Where's Mayu-shi?

[[Suzuha Amane]]: She cried herself out and went to sleep.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: I can understand that. She and Luka-shi were good friends.

[[Suzuha Amane]]: …Luka-nii-san.

Lukako was… dead.

He'd died protecting Mayuri and Faris.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …!

In my mind, his death overlapped with Mayuri's.

Mayuri's hands got colder.

And so did Lukako's.

It was the same no matter where I went.

Mayuri.

Lukako.

And Kurisu.

Everyone dies.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: A lot of people died after that war started.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: So many people… So many people died.

I knew this was coming.

At least, I thought I knew.

If I didn't reach Steins Gate.

If I stayed in the beta attractor field, the world would eventually burn.

I'd heard it a thousand times.

And I thought I knew what the future would look like.

but in the end, it was just a story about the distant future.

It didn't feel real at all.

I could only imagine it.

All I had were vague, unrealistic ideas.

All the thousands of nightmares I'd experienced…

I'd thought nothing could be worse than that.

But when I saw it with my own eyes, heard it with my own ears, and felt it with my own skin, I realized that everything I'd thought was wrong.

Piles of bodies.

Wild dogs devouring human corpses.

Dead soldiers.

The smell of burning flesh.

The smell of rotting organs.

The warmth of Lukako's blood in my hands.

The warmth of Mayuri's blood.

The warmth of Kurisu's blood.

I thought that If nothing else, I'd managed to save Mayuri.

I'd let Kurisu die, turned my eyes away from reality, and told myself that at least I'd saved Mayuri and the others.

But in the end, I hadn't saved anyone.

Even If I had, I'd only saved myself.

I was the only person I'd managed to save.

No one else.

Not Kurisu.

Not Mayuri.

Not Kagari. Not Lukako.

I hadn't saved anyone.*
>
