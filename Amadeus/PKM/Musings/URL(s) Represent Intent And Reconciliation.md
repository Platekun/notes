# URL(s) Represent Intent And Reconciliation

URL(s) represent the intent, and intent and correctness are not equal. A reconciliation is where you fix the route to match the proper state.

> *The URL is not the source of truth. It represents where the app wants to be, but not always where it should be. Reconciling these is tricky! (That's why I like thinking of route changes as events, not as state)*
