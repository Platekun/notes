# Find Your Own Answers

It is common in one’s life to feel lost, not knowing how to proceed regarding certain choice we have to make. When that happens we usually look for other’s advice but many times we do not attempt to find the answers within ourselves. We know better than anybody what should be done.

> *[[Sócrates (Peaceful Warrior)]]*: *Everyone tells you what's good for you. they don't want you to find your own answers. They want you to believe theirs.*
>
