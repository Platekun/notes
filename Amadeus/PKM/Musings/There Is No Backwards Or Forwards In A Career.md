# There Is No Backwards Or Forwards In A Career

A career is not only about climbing up a ladder, your career is part of your life and in life there is degrees of happiness.

> *There is no backwards or forwards in career, only degrees of happiness.*
> 
> 
> *If the old job is something that you enjoyed and were paid adequately then no reason to not go back if this new job isn't working out for you. Maybe you just found something that doesn't work for you and its time to take a hard look at what you really need.*
> 
> *I would see about a pay bump or at least a "bonus" to come back. If they are calling you then they are needing someone and you came to mind so you have value to them.*
>
