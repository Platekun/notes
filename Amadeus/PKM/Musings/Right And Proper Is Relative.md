# Right And Proper Is Relative

According to Kuzuki, what is right and is wrong does not depends on some external source but rather they are a relative concept according to the person in question.

> *[[Archer]]**:** I forgot to ask something. What is it that you consider right and proper?

[[Soichiro Kuzuki]]**:** Let me think... it is something that one does not regret, even if one has chosen wrongly. I view good and evil as having equal value, so that is the only evidence of humanity in me that I see as worthwhile.*
