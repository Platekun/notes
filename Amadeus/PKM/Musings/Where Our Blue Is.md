# Where Our Blue Is

I have been listening to this song for a while. I encountered this by change and It reminded me of my relationship with the one and only best friend I ever had: Simón.

I have flashbacks to my school times and how we drifted apart during the first year of college. Even after that everything changed and we didn't talk so much.

Similar to Satoru and Geto, we were opposites of each other and at the moment I am writing this, we ended up where the other was at the beginning. I used to struggle with academic subjects and he was a genius, now it's the opposite. Same with the job life. It's just like life can make multiple spins.

> *Desde esa ocasión en que pudimos todo compartir.
No pude notar que cada vez mas.
La gran maldición de ser diferentes se extendió.*
>
