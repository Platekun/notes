# What Is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a peer-to-peer electronic cash system. Bitcoin proceeded to be called the first [[What Is Crypto|cryptocurrency]] to be ever implemented. 

It was introduced in a [paper](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf) in 2009 by Satoshi Nakamoto. Nakamoto proposed Bitcoin as an solution a problem present on internet commerce, the heavy reliance on financial institutions: People use a trust based model where a third party is needed to perform a transaction however the cost of mediation increases its costs and limits the practical transaction size which limits the possibility of small casual transactions. It also has the flaws of having uncertainty because of reversible payments. These costs and payment uncertainties could be avoided by using fiat currency but this was a limited solution.

What Sakamoto proposed with Bitcoin was a solution to the problems caused by the usage of third-parties as a source of "*trust*”. That “t*rust*” was replaced by community trust in the form of a [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]].
