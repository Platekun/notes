# No. I will do it. Failure isn't an option

I think this is a clever way of hyping yourself up. Removing the optionality in situations leaves no choice for second thoughts and we can move into action.

> *Rainbow lights start drifting through the compartment.

It's time.

This is my Ragnarok.

I exit the time machine onto the familiar rooftop.

As before, it's empty. I can see the top of the train station a short distance away.

This is the third time I've been here now.

I run through the plan in my head one last time.

Time is short, but I have no choice. I have to do it.

No. I will do it. Failure isn't an option*
