# Should I Marry?

- What happens if my family dies (in 10-20 years)?
    - Current ages: Dad is 70, Aunt is 78, Mom is 64.
    - I'll be around 40-50 when this occurs (Mom will probably be the last).
    - Who I will have left?
        - Bogotá family.
        - Barranquilla family.
        - My school friends.
        - My colleagues.
    - What will happen to me?
        - I'd probably use work as an excuse to distract myself.
        - I can get really lonely.
        - I'd be a burden on them.
    - How will I spend my birthdays?
        - I don't know to be honest. I'd like to be with somebody but not alone.
    - How will I spend christmas?
        - I don't know to be honest. I'd like to be with somebody but not alone.
    - Who will remember me?
        - Everyone who cared about me will be dead.
- What do I want in life?
    - Reading Visual Novels.
    - Programming.
    - Working Out.
    - Occasional Beer.
- How do I picture myself in 5-10 years?
    - I'd be 35 years old.
    - I'd be somewhere higher in the corporate ladder.
    - I'd probably be working in the same line of action but with a higher salary.
- **What do I want for my life?**
    - A family (I'll lose mine sooner than later).
    - My family to be healthy.
    - Maintain my own health. Keep my physique while I can.
    - A healthy work-life balance.
    - Seek and apply knowledge (Programming, Finances, etc.).
    - Play a video game from time to time (Visual Novels are fun).
    - A salary that suffices “my family”.
- Do I ***TRUST*** this person? Am I at the stage where I learned not to doubt our relationship?
    - Yes:
        - Pros
            - We'd continue to the next step in our relationship.
            - Develop our union into something with more meaning.
                - I'll spend more time with the person I love the most.
        - Challenges
            - Decide on a compromise on engagement (Expected November 2024).
            - Decide on marriage timeline (Expected 2025).
            - Decide on kids timeline.
            - Set boundaries regarding in-laws coming over. Prevent invasion of our home.
            - Decide on a set of compromises:
                - Financial Plan (concrete goals, emergency fund, entertainment and personal expenses).
                    - We will probably need to save together for the wedding expenses.
                    - *“Money can break entire civilizations.”*
                    - *“It's just money.”*
                - Christmas.
                - Random holidays.
                - Family birthdays.
                - Friends / Colleagues birthdays.
                - Assisting weddings.
                - Explicit emotional expectations from each other:
                    - I want: Respect, peace, sex and food.
                    - She wants: ???
                - Write a set of rules we need to live by, something like a decalogue (This can be done little by little).
                - Onboard Lourdes in house logistics:
                    - Bath cleaning.
                    - Kitchen cleaning.
                    - Laundry (Weekly, monthly, Ocasional).
                    - Keeping the house clean with little habits.
    - No:
        - Consequences:
            - We break up.
            - Will need some time to rest before looking for a next relationship.
            - I'm not in the best position to find a new partner.
            - I'll need to look actively for a new partner if I desire one.
            - I'm an introvert which has some troubles.
            

My aunt did say I should forgive her for her rudeness and naivety. It was her upbringing what caused Lourdes to behave this way. However this problem won't be solved until we agreed upon the basis that we trust each other.
