# Redefine Your Relation With Information

The key part here is “a tool for thinking”, nowadays I just feel more technology in my life doesn't automatically mean it's useful. But how can I make use of my tech to *think?*. What ARE my goals?

> *When you transform your relationship to information, you will being to see the technology in your life not just as a storage medium but as a tool for thinking. Like a bicycle for the mind, once we learn how to use it properly, technology can enhance our cognitive abilities and accelerate us toward our goals far faster than we could ever achieve on our own.*
>
