# The World As A Video Game

First of all: Clever foreshadowing Takumi's nature. Second, seeing real life as a game where everyone is a character is an interesting idea. The concepts of gamification apply to some extend to real life aspect like our character growth, and interestingly it connect to the idea that characters in a game can only [[Suffering And Anger Are Necessary|progress as they struggle]].

This piece also hints the escapism sentiment I felt when I faced a boredom situation with the [[The Illusion That Time Has Stopped|Walmart client]].

> *It all felt like a lie.

The whole world was filled with fiction.

It was filled with evil, evil that was out to get me.

[[Yua Kusonoki]]: Log in to the chatroom you always use, and see for yourself.

[[Yua Kusonoki]]: You'lll see that I'm telling the truth.

[[Yua Kusonoki]]: And…

[[Yua Kusonoki]]: Everything I said… I meant. If nothing else… I want you to believe that.

After her bizarre speech, she added that last sentence, and then left.

Of course, I couldn't believe her. How could I believe her when she'd lied to me?

The “everything I was” that she'd shown me was something I refused to accept, no matter what. I wanted to believe there was no way I could be Shogun.

But from the timestamps on the chat log, it was clear that the conversation didn't make sense.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Was I …sleepwalking?

I'd been at @Café, sure. But I hadn't been asleep. I couldn't have been sleepwalking.

Then why didn't I have any memories at all of writing these comments as ”Shogun"?

The very idea was ridiculous.

I decided what I needed to escape from al lthe lies was some good old ESO, my other fictional world.

In ESO, everything was a lie. It was just a game, after all.

But in Baselard, I could be KnightHeart, a god-like being.

If only the lies and fictions could be like this. I could accept that reality. I wanted to be here forever.

What's wrong with that?

Or maybe…

ESO's world was the real one, and this one was the fake. This one was the game.

Takumi Nishijou was just a fictional character, and my true identity was KnightHeart.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Hey, KnightHeart…

I called out to the palading on the screen.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Am I controlling you, or are you controlling me? are you the real person, and I'm the avatar?

What if the world I'm in is a game? If that was the case, it would explain everything.

If somebody was staring at me right now, just like how I was staring at KnightHeart…

Whose eyes are those?

The answer to that question would be “the player controlling me”.

If that was the case, then I wish, they'd stop sucking at the game so much, but an avatar doesn't get to complain.

It would explain how Shogun and I could be the same person.

It was like with Liselotte. Liselotte and KnightHeart couldn't exist in Baselard at the same time. They both had the same player: me.

Maybe in the same way, Takumi Nishijou and Shogun couldn't both exist in this world at the same time.

Or maybe it was like a bug.

Speaking of bugs, maybe these strange women appearing and the murder cases were all bugs, too.

The images Shogun sent me flashed through my mind, and I winced, I shook my head desperately.

I wanted to run away from everything.

If there is a player controlling me… then please, reset the game.

Just start over from character creation.*
