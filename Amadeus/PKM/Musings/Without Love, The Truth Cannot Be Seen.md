# Without Love, The Truth Cannot Be Seen

Without trust, reasoning cannot be done. what is she feeling right now? She's feeling we broke up.

2016: I met her on TICOM, at first sight I though she was really intelligent because we said she was a tester. Love didn't come at first sight but rather it developed. I remember discussing lots of things with her, I also laughed more when I was interacting with her. We were together until I left the company, that's when I presented her to my mom as my girlfriend. We kept in contact after leaving, she worked in TICOM while I focused in my studies. We used to walk a lot around town as we didn't have enough money. Money wasn't an issue back then, I didn't care. She even stood with me while I looked for a new job at the end of the year. She was jobless as her contract with TICOM ended. We supported each other in the meantime.

2017: At the start of the year I was hired by NativApps, she wasn't exactly candid about me getting hired but not because of envy but rather because of her frustration. I understood that, despite making me feel really sad (I like sharing achievements with her). I think this was the time we started to have our first fight. I wasn't sure she understood me back then, I feel like a freak when social anxiety hits me hard. But despite that the year went through.
