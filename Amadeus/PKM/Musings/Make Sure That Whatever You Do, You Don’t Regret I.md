# Make Sure That Whatever You Do, You Don’t Regret It

I really like the piece of advise that Kunosato tried to gave Miyashiro. Decisions always come with their consequences, so making the decision itself is a personal thing no matter what everyone else says. Everyone is entitled to their our decision but bearing the consequences comes with taking that decision.

> *It was decided that Shinjo would drive me to the Hirakiwo.*

*I wanted him to take the others to akihabara as soon as possible, but I didn't see how could I get through the area around Shibuya station on my own.

[[Mio Kunosato]] : Miyashiro!

[[Mio Kunosato]]: You've got your phone, right?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: …?

I put my hand inside my pocket to check.

Of course, it was there.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: I do…why?

[[Mio Kunosato]] : If you find anything on the Committee, no matter what, contact me immediately.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: …

I chuckled. That was what I'd expected her to say.

I didn't know if I'd find anything, but I promised that I would.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Onoe told me to get you out of Shibuya.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: I agreed, because she told me that if I got the psychics out of Shibuya, it could get me closer to the Committee.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Yeah.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: But if you decide not to go, there's no reason for me to stop you. I have no special need for you in particular. As long as I have Yamazoe and Arimura, that's enough.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Yeah, that's right.

She was correct.

If getting a psychic out of Shibuya would cause something to happen…Uki and Arumira would do just as well as I would.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: So we’re not waiting for you, okay?

Her words seemed to be testing my resolve, one last time.

So I looked dead in the eye and I answered.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: That’s fine, I understand that.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: …

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: …?

But after that for a while, she said nothing

She seemed to be, for once, at a loss for words.

I frowned.

Did she not like that answer?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Is something wrong?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: About what you were saying before.

She finally opened her mouth, hesitantly.

She was probably talking about the call I’d gotten from Serika.

When she’d told me that she was the one who killed my parents

[[Mio Kunosato]]: If that’s true.

What? Did you figure something out?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: …No.

Kunosato probably found Serika’s words strange, just like I did.

They didn’t make sense.

And Kunosato probably had a theory on her own - and one she was relunctlant to share.

But in the end - she put her hand on my shoulder, without saying a word.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Huh?

Until now, all she’d ever done was grab me or choike me, so I was surprised.

I never expected this.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Either way, you should find the answer for yourself. Make sure that whatever you do, you don’t regret it.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: …

And the words she said were different than usual, too.

Kunosato was saying something nice to me, a psychic?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: What?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Huh? Oh, um, no…

I was so surprised that I said something I shouldn't have.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: I…I'd rather you sent me off with a last goodbye from Kei. I was a listener.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Hell no. I refuse to play the role of that goody-goody suck-up when I'm not trying to manipulate information. Get going.

She pushed me away with the hand she'd placed on my shoulder.

I suddenly felt embarrased.

Why had I said that?

I was so embarrased that I tried to hurry into the car.

Kunosato looked at me and said,

”If you really want to hear Kei again, wait till the next broadcast.”

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: …

I turned around and looked at her face one more time.

Her features were elegant.

and her beautifol, almond-shaped eyes were always frozen cold. They never betrayed what she was really thinking.

Her personality was cool as ice, befitting a dedicated scientist who was always willing to do whatever it took to achieve her goal, regardless of the cost.

But sometimes I caught a glimpse of some burning passion inside her…

She was really a mysterious girl.

Oh right. There was one last thing I had to ask her…

Since this might really be my last chance.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: What? Why are you staring at me?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Kunosato…what's your power?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: What?

The distance between us started to shrink, but it grew back with that one question.

I felt all the air around me freeze.

I panicked.

I…I mean, I saw it! In your room.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Saw what?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: A Di-Sword. That was yours, right?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Huh…?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Hah…Hahah…

Just as I thought things between us would end with another series of cold insults from her, Kunosato started to laugh.

She kept laughing in a low voice as I stood there with my mouth hanging open.

All I could do was stand there and watch.

I didn't know Kunosato could laugh like that.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Are you really a Gigalomaniac? You can't even tell the difference between the real thing and a fake.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: A fake?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: That's just a replica I have for research purposes. My lab in America made it based off some documents from six years ago.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: It was an attempt to find out why, even if the sahpes may vary, all the pipes to the Dirac sea take the form of a sword. But i didn't work, so they gave me the useless piece of junk.

[[Mio Kunosato]]: It's not good for anything but taking up space…but I do use it as a way to hang things up to dry.

And then she laughed again.

After she was done laughing, she pushed me into the car.

And before she closed the door.

Oh, right. Your classmate - Itou, was it? He looks like he'lll survive

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: Oh!

[[Mio Kunosato]]: You seem to have forgotten, but you promised to be my guinea pig in exchange.

Come back and keep your promise.

Then she closed the door.

I nodded from behind the window and mouthed the words, "Thank you for everything.”

Everyone really was trying to make me feel better.

Everyone really did want to see me again.

But now I was going alone down a path that meant that might never happen.*
