# What Is Ethereum?

Ethereum is an open source [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]] with [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is An API|programable functionalities]]. It has its own cryptocurrency called Ether (also known as ETH) that is used in within its network in order to function.

Ethereum was introduced in a [paper](https://ethereum.org/669c9e2e2027310b6b3cdce6e1c52962/Ethereum_Whitepaper_-_Buterin_2014.pdf) in 2014 by Vitalik Buterin and was later launched in 2015.  The paper explores the history of [[What Is Crypto|cryptocurrency]] and [[What Is Bitcoin|Bitcoin]]. Ethereum was proposed as a general purpose blockchain along with its fundamental concepts.

What Vitalik proposed with Ethereum paper was an analysis of some aspects he considered they could make a [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]] something more than just a [[What Is Bitcoin|digital money system]]. Concepts like [[What Is Bitcoin|Bitcoin]] could be improve on like the possibility of alternative coins, protocols on top of a hypothetical blockchain, a proper programming language, etc.

A key difference between the Ethereum and [[What Is Bitcoin|Bitcoin]] blockchains is that [[What Is Bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is only a payment platform (like [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/)) while Ethereum was designed to be [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is An API|programmable]]. It has the ability to store [[What Is A Smart Contract|computer programs]] that can be executed when a [message](https://app.notion.com/p/8ad83899b7164e58bf7efa2f1202d3a8?pvs=21) is received.

This ability to be [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is An API|programmable]] opens up areas of [banking services](https://app.notion.com/p/6c0fe6e19f0a47a1a0d218736c687175?pvs=21), [NFT](https://app.notion.com/p/a9c60131a71f4570a3679b9cf77c85b6?pvs=21)(s) among others.
