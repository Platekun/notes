# State Charts For Business Concerns

Identify different business concerns inside your application and create state charts for them. Components will subscribe and send events to them. Something similar to one what would do with MobX stores.

> *I would argue that charts should be aligned with your business domains and not with visual components. If you’re building a registration app say, you could have a SignUp chart, a SignIn chart and a Payment chart. These 3 could still be parallel within the same single chart.*
