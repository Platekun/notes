# You Will Not Convince Everyone Always

This is something similar to what was developed in “*The Structure of Scientific Revolutions***”. Good ideas are not always enough, instead there has to be an amount of time so the new** wave of believers can take on and create a new paradigm while old  believers die of old age.

> *Note to self: no matter how good some technical concept is, you'll never convince 100% of developers that it's a good idea (nor is it a good idea 100% of the time).
Optimize for developers who want to learn more and be convinced; not the ones who are extremely opposed.*
