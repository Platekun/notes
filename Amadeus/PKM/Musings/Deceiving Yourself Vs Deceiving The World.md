# Deceiving Yourself Vs Deceiving The World

Okabe's situation is clearly that one where you are warned about the dangers of going a certain way and the only way for us to wake up is to have a sneak peak of what is ahead of us in that path.

> *[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …

Kurisu - she was still trapped inside that world.

Inside that sterile, lifeless world that froze you to the depths of your soul.

Even after her death, her memories were still alive.

And there were all kinds of conspiracies forming around her living memories.

Even after she lost her body. Kurisu's pain continued.

No, it wasn't just Kurisu.

Kagari, and Suzuha.

Everyone was suffering, everyone was miserable, but still they tried to move forward.

I thought that even if the Third World War came, no matter what happened to the world, as long as Mayuri was happy, that was all that mattered.

I tried to think that.

I thought that no matter how much blood and tears awaited us in the future, as long as things were fine now, that was all that mattered.

I thought I'd understood.

I thought I'd understood how awful the future that Suzuha described was.

And how hard Daru and suzuha would have to fight. I thought I understood all that.

Suzuha, firing her gun and staining her hands with blood like it was nothing at all.

Mayuri and Faris, pale and colorless.

Lukako lying on the ground and never getting up again.

Kagari and the others, whose lives were ruined by fate.

I'd seen them with my own eyes and touched them with my own hands, and then I finally understood.

I'd been only pretending to know, so I could run away.

I'd told myself that I was going to die in 2025, and looked the other way.

2025 -

I wasn't dead.

Everyone just thought so.

Everyone had been deceived.

That's right, deceived.

Me in the past,

and everyone else in the future.

The world - was deceived.

I thought the future was settled.

But a different future awaited me.
