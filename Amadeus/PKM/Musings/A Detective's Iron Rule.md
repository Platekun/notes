# A Detective's Iron Rule

I really like this pieces of wisdom Ban throws here and there. I think I really like detective characters as I can resonate with that way of thinking about problem solving. This is specially useful when facing a blocker that seems to be unsolvable.

> *[[Ban Yasuji]]: We might want to look into any companies or groups connected with them.

[[Katsuko Momose]]: Already done.

[[Ban Yasuji]]: That’s my Momo, always quick on the draw.

[[Katsuko Momose]]: So?

Momose raised up her hands and stretched. She tilted her head a little, and you could hear the sound of her neck popping.

[[Katsuko Momose]]: Nothing. A dead end.

[[Ban Yasuji]]: Does that mean your theory was wrong?

[[Katsuko Momose]]: It might, actually.

[[Katsuko Momose]]: I spent a lot of time on it, too. Time I should’ve spent on paying work.

[[Katsuko Momose]]: But I hit a dead end. Either it’s extremely well-hidden, or like Suwa said, I was simply mistaken.

[[Ban Yasuji]]: Hmm…

[[Ban Yasuji]]: When a cop runs into a situation like this, there’s an iron rule: Approach the problem from a different angle.

Suwa: Do you have any ideas?

[[Ban Yasuji]]: who do you think would benefit if the GE rate went up on the weekends?*
>
