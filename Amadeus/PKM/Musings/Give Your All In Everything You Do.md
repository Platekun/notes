# Give Your All In Everything You Do

Despite this being a clear manipulation of [[Serika Onoe]]  to tempt [[Takuru Miyashiro]]  to solve the case, I do agree on this philosophy on giving everything to what I do in my life. This could relate somehow to how I think I should [[I Am The Captain Of My Soul|live as a protagonist]] in my life. We need to find love in everything we do.
