# Hot Saturday

Spent my morning without electricity, Barranquilla is becoming like a hot oven. 

Despite the rough start I was able to enjoy some time reading in the morning and my parents helped me in the afternoon with the market.

Lourdes arrived really late on night but we managed to share some time and est dinner together. Sadly she was too tired because the travel.

We spoke about our suffering when we were in college. I told her that college taught me an instinct about surviving anything.
