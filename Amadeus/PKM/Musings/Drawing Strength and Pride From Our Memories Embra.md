# Drawing Strength and Pride From Our Memories: Embracing Mistakes and Sacrifices

We can draw strength, confidence and pride if we can remember where we come from. Our memories, they all meant something and that includes our mistakes and sacrifices.

> *You must not reject the three weeks you spent drifting between worldlines.

You must not undo the past.

It all meant something.

Every mistake. Every sacrifice.

Without them, I would not be standing here now.

From that knowledge, I draw strength, confidence, and pride. The memories I shoulder deserve nothing less.

And so, I am no longer afraid.

I will reach the unknown worldline, Steins Gate.

The future is uncertain. But that is how it should be.*
