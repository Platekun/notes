# Availability Zones

Data centers are organized into availability zones (AZs). [[Regions]] contain at least three AZs and AZs at least contain one data center.

Similar to [[Regions|regions]], AZs also have their unique names (e.g, `ap-southeast-2a`, `ap-southeast-2b` and `ap-southeast-2c`).
