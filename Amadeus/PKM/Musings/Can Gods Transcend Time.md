# Can Gods Transcend Time?

> [[Kurisu Makise]]: Once you go to the Beta worldline, the 'me’ that your brain perceives will die.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: In other words, your perspective will have chosen the worldline where I die instead of the one where I don't. However…

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Why can't the world your brain perceives be different from the world my brain perceives?

Brains. I forgot her specialty was neuroscience.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: So you're saying worldlines are independent?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: But we knew Suzuha…

[[Kurisu Makise]]: There's no way to prove that she was Hashida's daughter, or anything she said about 2036 either. Not without going there ourselves.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: And if Reading Steiner is what you say, then it means that your subjective experience influences the memories of every single human being.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: That's completely absurd, Okabe. If it's true, then you're literally a god.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: But there are no gods in reality.
