# Keep Moving Forward While You Worry

> *[[Rowen Ilbert]]: Are you certain this is a good idea?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Yes. I have a responsibility to my mission... To the world.

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: And you wish to remain true to that burden.

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: You are such a strong, proud woman. Seeing your determination, it reopens some old wounds.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: What do you mean?

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: Lord Cline tasked me with saving this country. His dying words have weighed heavily on my mind lately.

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: Am I even capable of such a task? Can I put a stop to Nachtigal's schemes?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Back at Fort Gandala, it seemed like he knew you.

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: He is a friend of mine. One of my oldest.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: So that's the source of your concern... Whether you can fight against a friend or not.

[[Teepo]]: No way! If he was a real friend, you wouldn't fight 'im!

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Fretting over it won't strengthen your resolve. Only willpower can do that.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: For now, will you come with us?

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: I'm sorry?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: You can still grapple with these issues if you like, but a human life is short. Time is of the essence.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: You might as well keep moving forward while you worry.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Hesitation is a common human failing, after all

[[Jude Mathis]]: Why not come along, Rowen? I'd feel safer with you around anyway.

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: Hohoho! Your words ring true. Caving to worry and doubt would only waste my time, and this old man is not growing any younger.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Does that mean--

[[Rowen Ilbert]]: Yes, please allow me to accompany you.

[[Elize Lutus]]: I'll come too!*
