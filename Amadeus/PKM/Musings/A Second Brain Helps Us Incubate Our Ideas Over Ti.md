# A Second Brain Helps Us Incubate Our Ideas Over Time

I've been recently thinking that I am really prone to “*recency bias*”. Sometimes an idea pops into my brain at the last phase of the work-day and I really push to implement it. It's only recently where I have stopped myself from doing this and letting the idea incubate at least for another day to see if there's even a simpler way of doing things. Don't believe the first thought you have.

> *Even when we do a brainstorm, that still relies only on ideas that we can think up right now. What are the chances that the most creative, most innovative approaches will instantly be top of mind? What are the odds that the best way to move forward is one of the first ways we come up with?*
