# Hidden Character Growth

I have thought about this in two occasions:

- When [[Takuru Miyashiro]] got separated from [[Serika Onoe]], she didn’t understand why his desires had changed.
- When [[Milla Maxwell]] reunited again with [[Jude Mathis]] after her death. She noticed Jude’s developed while she wasn’t around.

> *Our character evolves independently, which is why sometimes others may struggle to comprehend the motivations behind our actions or attitudes when they reconnect with us.*
