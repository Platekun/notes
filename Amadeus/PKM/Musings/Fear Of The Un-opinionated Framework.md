# Fear Of The Un-opinionated Framework

- When starting projects always look for a better solution of the tool you previously have used before.
- In case you find yourself in an unknown land, it could be more helpful to look for opinionated frameworks over un-opinionated ones.
- Opinionated frameworks are not absolute truths but try them out in order to draw comparisons later.
- The feeling that an un-opinionated framework can give is "*fear*" because you are left with the burden of growing and maintaining the architecture.
- Sharing a common vision and conventions between developers is something that has to be considered when working for longevity and projects that grow fast. Simple projects do not have to fear of this, so it's ok to go with un-opinionated frameworks.
- Opinionated is not an insult, you can think of it as a healthy alternative to a total anarchy.
- Find rational opinions, they should be established or well thought practices.
