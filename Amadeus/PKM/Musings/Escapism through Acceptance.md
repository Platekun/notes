# Escapism through Acceptance

Robotic;Notes protagonist shared this when pursuing the decision of building the GunBuild-2 Robot even if everyone was against it. Kaito mentioned this when Akiho got scolded by her classmates.

It is interesting because “*Accepting things as they come*” is usually how the stoic saying goes. One must progress by accepting reality. But what if someone had the will / power / capability to change one’s fate? That would be considered escapism then because you are running away from a situation you can change but is a pain to go through.

This resonates with the Robotic;Notes text bellow the logo that goes: *"The science itself may prove cynical. However, one mustn't forget that there is a scientific element in all things. The important truth is this: I am the master of my fate, I am the captain of my soul."*

> *There is no point in freaking out now.

We just gotta accept things as they come.

Though I’m sure some people would refer to that as escaping from reality.*
