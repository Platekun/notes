# The Magpie Developer

- Developers are like magpies in the sense they are distracted by shiny new things to decorate their nest with.
- The way things change is like a stream, it's relentless and we are inundated with shiny new things.
- There are so many new things that the concept of "new" does not stays for long because there is already another new thing out.
- "*Eventually, you grow weary of the endless processing of shiny new things*".
- The "*shiny new thing*" lifecycle:
    1. Elite developers notice something can be improved upon their workflow (Be a programming language, model or paradigm) and start looking for something that can distinguish them better from their colleagues.
    2. Elite developers do their homework and create their "shiny new thing" that improves their workflow.
    3. Elite developers start evangelizing about the "shiny new thing" and sub-elite developers follow the elite developers by creating material, guides, posts and accelerate the development and testing of the "shiny new thing".
    4. The rest of developers notice the "shiny new thing" and start buying material and take courses to learn.
    5. Repeat step 1.
- A magpie developer loves change to the detriment of his own craft.
- Keep you and your users happy with the technology you choose. Nothing else matters.
- Be selective in the pursuit of shiny and new and you may improve as a developer.
