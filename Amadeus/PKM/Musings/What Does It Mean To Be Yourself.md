# What Does It Mean To Be Yourself?

Yesterday we went to Valentina’s dinner. It was a formal event however it also had a stage to dance for a while thanks to the DJ.

Lately I have been thinking about who am I and how should I act. I been feeling like Everyone is telling me how to behave. I felt out of place when my family was having a good time speaking with others however I felt alone despite the fact I shared sometime with Lourdes.

The feeling of disconnection between me and everyone else I tried to speak to felt abysmal. I didn’t know what to talk about with Lourdes’ sister however my father was able to connect almost inmediatly.

What’s going on here? The entire time I was feeling bored and like I really didn’t care for everyone there.

Am I socially awkward or just bored? I know people behave different in different enviroments, For example, I don’t get along with the girls there as we had our encounters time ago and I didn’t want to establish a conversation there.

I feel my family thought badly of my behavior but what is exactly is bas behavior? Not being the star of the night like my mother? I’m not my mother nor I want to be her.

I just want to be myself, I just want to feel good with myself. I don’t want to follow somebody’s way of Life. Said that, I feel that being muself results in a socially inept person at the eyes of extroverted people.

What does it mean to be yourself? Who is the man I want to become?
