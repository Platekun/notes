# Managed Databases

The term “*managed*” refers to letting a cloud provider take all the responsibility of what it means [[Self-Hosted Databases|hosting our own database]] with the tradeoff that we will lose the amount of control we have over it.

There are valid reasons to take this approach like reducing the complexity of the system and not wanting to deal with heavy lifting of a database.
