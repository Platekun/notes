# Types Of Blockchains

Depending on the feature set of the [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]], there are multiple categories in four types of blockchains:

- **Public:** [[What Is A Blockchain|Bockchains]] that have no restrictions to be accessed. The only requirement is internet access. These type of [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchains]] are the ones that are known for their [[What Is Bitcoin|economic rewards]].
- **Private:** [[What Is A Blockchain|Blockchains]] that cannot be joined unless invited by its administrators (Governors of the network). Participation and validation is restricted.
- **Hybrid:** [[What Is A Blockchain|Blockchains]] that feature characteristics of centralization and [[What Is A Dristributed Database|decentralization]]. Which characteristics depends on the specific blockchain.
- **Sidechains:** [[What Is A Blockchain|Blockchains]] that are separated and run in parallel from a “*main*” [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]], their entries can be linked to and from the sidechain. It is usually used for high-volume of transactions.
