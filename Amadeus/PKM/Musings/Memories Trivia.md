# Memories Trivia

> *[[Shiina Kagari]]: Memories are controlled by a part of the brain called the hippocampus. And there's a special part of the hippocampus called the fascia dentata.

[[Shiina Kagari]]: When nerve cells are constructed there, they cause existing neurological pathways to reorganize. That's what makes you forget things.

[[Shiina Kagari]]: And so when this study team did experiments on mice, they saw false memories created.*
