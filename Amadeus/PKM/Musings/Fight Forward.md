# Fight Forward

I woke up today feeling really bad, I suffered from a severe headache due to stress however that didn't stop me from doing cardio and doing my daily routine. I must face one of those stressful tasks at work, the worst part of it is that it's a race against time to deliver one of the feature my client needs to ship quickly.

> *The sound of the Gion Shoja bells echoes the impermanence of all things; the color of the sala flowers reveals the truth that the prosperous must decline HOWEVER we are the exception*
> 

This poem has been floating around my head recently, I like to think about myself as the exception to the rule and not the rule itself. If I were to rewrite this into my situation I'd say:

*The sound of the slack notification echoes the dreadful feeling of shipping things at all cost. The amount of tech debt reveals the truth that the prosperous codebase must decline HOWEVER we are the exception.*

Fight, wolf. You must fight, together or alone, you must survive any challenge. Steel your mind and move forward. When the storm passes you will see how much you have advanced.
