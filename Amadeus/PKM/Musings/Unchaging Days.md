# Unchaging Days

Robotic;Notes’s protagonist mentioned this when playing Kill-Ballad (a fighting game) on its favorite park of the island. The scene describes how the sky looks reddish due to the location of the Tanegashima island.

[[Kaito Yashio]] describes “*ordinary*” and “*unchanging*” days. I think I could say that we can create a loop in our life somehow. Repeating the same events over and over can create [‣](https://app.notion.com/p/551a063d099e4a74a8744a6b8636b626?pvs=21).

> *But now, I’m totally used to seeing it. I don’t really feel one way or the other about it.

That color in the sky is just another part of my everyday life.

My ordinary, unchanging days.

My life on this island, repeating endlessly.

It’s hard to believe it’ll all be coming to and end in six months.

But for now, I’ll just focus on getting home and eating dinner.*
>
