# We Don't Know How The Other One Feels

This conversantion between Kunosato and Momose, resonated with me because when I read this I stopped saying "*I know how you feel*” to others. I really don't know how they feel and I don't want to tell lies. Instead I always try to be blunt about not knowing but understanding how their feelings make them feel the way they do.

> *[[Katsuko Momose]] **:** That was a little harsh, don’t you think?

[[Mio Kunosato]] **:** It’s the truth.

“That’s not what I meant”, Momose began.

[[Katsuko Momose]] **:** You know what it’s like to lose someone close to you, too.

[[Mio Kunosato]] **:** …

Kunosato looked down, as if searching her own feelings.

But then she looked up like nothing had happened, and turned back to her computer.

[[Mio Kunosato]]  I only care about myself.

[[Mio Kunosato]] **:** The idea of a boy and a girl who’ve both lost the ones they’ve loved consoling one another disgusts me, I don’t give sympathy, and I don’t want it , either.

[[Katsuko Momose]] **:** I think that sympathy is part of being kind, you know.

[[Mio Kunosato]] **:** And I think there’s nothing that pushes a person away so much as saying “I know how you feel, so hang in there.”.

[[Katsuko Momose]] **:** You don’t have to say anything. People can just tell.

[[Katsuko Momose]] **:** Being able to do that is part of being an adult.

Kunosato chuckled as she looked at the screen.*
