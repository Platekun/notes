# Show, Don’t Order

Robotic;Notes’ protagonist mentioned this when talking to the other protagonist about certain information she could find useful.

This kind of thinking Kaito has could be beneficial if applied in real life context. I certainly do not want others to think I boss them around. I like showing people a path, regardless or not they choose to walk through it. If I prevent myself from creating and expectation I can avoid disappointments. The most important thing is showing a path and not directing the other person around.

> *What she does with the info is out of my hands.

My role is just to show her where the path lies. Whether she walks that path or not is entirely up to her.*
