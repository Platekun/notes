# Pricing

[[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is AWS|AWS]] service useage generally cost us money. They offer a variaty of pricing and payment models depending on the actual service mused.

**In Advance**

The idea is to commit to a certain amount of usage.

We are required to pay for a reserved capacity with the benefit of a discounted price.

**On Demand**

The idea is paying only for the resources used.

The specific costs of the resources used vary depending on the service involved.

**Subscription**

The idea is paying a monthly / yearly fee for a specific service (e.g, The support plan).

## AWS Free Tier

For new registrations [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is AWS|AWS]] offers a generous benefit to gain experience with their platform and services.

We are granted services with free trials, services that are free for the next 12 months of our account creation and services that are always free as long as we do not surpass a quota.
