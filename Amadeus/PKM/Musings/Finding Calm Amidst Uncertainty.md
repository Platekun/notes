# Finding Calm Amidst Uncertainty

This insight from [[Okabe Rintaro]] is really interesting as I have found myself in those situations sometimes. I cannot understand exactly how to trigger that emotion of being clam while being inside a storm. I actually reminds me about [[Sócrates (Peaceful Warrior)]]: “Take Out The Trash”.

> *[[Suzuha Amane]]: We're counting on you, Uncle.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …

Instead of answering, I close my eyes.

I strangely find myself calm.

No impatience. No tension. None of the anxiety I felt the first time.

How many times have I tasted failure?

My hubris hurt those closest to me. My foolishness cost them their dreams.

And even though those mistakes have been undone, the memories remain as brand on my heart.*
