# What Dreams Are Made Of

![Untitled](What%20Dreams%20Are%20Made%20Of/Untitled.png)

## Learned Lessons

- People need to work towards something (dreams, projects or goals). Not having anything can cause boredom which eventually could lead into [[The Illusion That Time Has Stopped|depression]] and emptiness.
- Be confident and master your skills. There will be a time when it will be key to show the world how reliable you are.
- An inferiority complex is both a blessing and a curse: It can drive our motivation just because we want to prove something but It can also break us if we let it get to our head.
