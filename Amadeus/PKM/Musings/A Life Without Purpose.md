# A Life Without Purpose

I remember than when [[Takuru Miyashiro]] tried to re-create [[Serika Onoe]] as a new person, she seemed really scared about living her life without reading other people’s thoughts and without a clear purpose. She didn’t understand how people could smile and laugh like that.

This is interesting as that is the way people usually live, they are taken where the wind guides them and for someone who has clear objectives this feels horrible.
