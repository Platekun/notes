# Rising Above Resentment

> *[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Now that you're published in “Science”, expect a lot more of this. You need to practice.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: …I'd rather not.

Kurisu whispered to herself.

Maho didn't like being around lots of other people either, so she knew how Kurisu felt.

But there were a lot of scientists who wanted to be published in a major journal, then go on to give public lectures.

And she'd beaten out many of them to win that honor. People were jelous.

She needed to fulfill her responsibilities, even if just to shut them up.*
