# Defying Fate

![Untitled](Defying%20Fate/Untitled.png)

## Learned Lessons

- Everything occurs for a reason (Whether it was decided by god or another unknown force). Despite that, everyone can decide how to act upon these events, that what we call “*Free will*” (”*I refuse to give up*”).
- The past is part of ourselves, we cannot get rid of our mistakes however we can use it to fuel your future actions (“*Keep the past, for all intents and purposes, where it is*”).
- Difficult problems can become solvable by using a different perspective (“*Deceive yourself, deceive the world*”).
