# Notes As Knowledge Building Blocks

A note therefore is our own unique perspective of *something,* that something can be anything as long as its digital it can be stored in a note. As long as we add our own perspective to it, it will be helpful in the future.

> *For modern, professional notetaking, a note is a “knowledge building block” - a discrete unite of information interpreted through your unique perspective and stored outside your head.

By this definition, a note could include a passage from a book or article that you were inspired by; a photo or image form the web with your annotations; or a bullet-point list of your meandering thoughts on a topic, among many other examples. A note could include a single quote from a film that really struck you, all the way to thousan of words you saved from an in-depth book. The length and format doesn't matter according to your taste, translated into your own words, or drawn from your life experience, and stored in a secure place, then it qualifies as a note.*
