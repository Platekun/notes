# I Just Wanted To Be Stronger

I just wanted to be stronger, stronger than I am and overcome my sadness. However lately I have been feeling I turned my back to my past. I always wanted to hide it so nobody sees it again. I don't know how I shoud I act, I feel awkward around her because I fee out of place always. I feel I should hide my feelings more to hide anger.
