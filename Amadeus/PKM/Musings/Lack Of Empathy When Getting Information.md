# Lack Of Empathy When Getting Information

This feels familiar to what we usually see when people are watching the news. You see people doing all kind of comments regarding people who live through certain situations like they know everything and applying their prejudices. This sickens me, however I also do that to a certain extent.

[[Takuru Miyashiro]] **:** I was looking for information on the internet and couldn't help myself.

But as I started to become a part of the murders, I began to realize how irresponsible and insensitive the information disseminated on the internet was, and how awful real life could be.

It was karma…I'd once been proud of my status as a right-sider, but now the whole thing felt like a punishment.*
