# Information Clash Instinct

I guess this applies to everyone every time they are shown they are wrong in some regard. Nobody likes being wrong however sometimes this conflict is unavoidable. Someone has to be right and the other has to be wrong sometimes. Understanding our wrongs from rights is the first step to [[The Ultimate Goal Of A Right-Sider|being a right-sider]].
