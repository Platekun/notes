# Hey Future Me

I haven't written here since about two weeks ago. Since then Christmas happened…I tried to share christmas with my family however I had a problem with my father as he was stuck with his phone on that day. The following days after Christmas I was really angry I decided to stay alone for while. Now more calmed I decided to just act as if nothing happened. I just want to share with my family on this end of year.

I also followed some advice from Tiago Forte about doing an annual review yesterday. I decided to spend the last day doing some cleaning as I don't want to focus on that for the very first thing in my new year (I think I will need the cleaning lady to come twice a week once Lourdes moves in).

With this year coming to an end, I think It's been a pretty good ride and hopefully this new one will be even better. I need to focus on the things I want to do but I also need to share more moments with Lourdes and her family. This year I understood that I'm not much different from her.

Something that stuck with me was “*Stories happen in the ordinary of everyday life.*“, this is just a round around way of saying my life depends on me.

Hey old me…You are going to be fine, I know you are scared at this moment because you are anxious. There will be times where you'll want to cry and that is okay. As everything in life, this is only temporary. Calmer times will come. While the future seems bad right now, not everything will be bad, try to remember you are not alone. Just try to manage your temper a bit more, okay? It really sucks hurting those who you love.

Hey future me…How did I do? Please watch over me.
