# Lack Of Mental Clarity

> [[Alvin]]*: Damn it.

[[Alvin]]: Hey, just go ahead and kill me already.

[[Jude Mathis]]: ......!

[[Jude Mathis]]: Kill you? How can you even say that to me?!

[[Jude Mathis]]: Milla saved your rotten life! Don't be so eager to just throw it 
away!

[[Jude Mathis]]: Don't you get it?! Milla died because she wanted us to live!

[[Jude Mathis]]: She didn't die so we would-- So we'd end up like this.

[[Alvin]]: Well then, what the hell should we be doing?!

[[Jude Mathis]]: I'm not Milla! I can't live like her!

[[Alvin]]: I don't have a mission to fulfill!

[[Jude Mathis]]: She's not here to hold our hands anymore. We have to think for 
ourselves now.

[[Alvin]]: Think for ourselves how?!

[[Jude Mathis]]: No one is gonna make our decisions for us anymore!

[[Jude Mathis]]: And no one... No one will take responsibility for our actions 
ever again!

[[Alvin]]: ......

[[Jude Mathis]]: Even if Milla's mission was all a lie…

[[Jude Mathis]]: The truth is, she was still willing to put her life on the line to 
fulfill it.

[[Jude Mathis]]: It's not a matter of if you can or can't. It's a matter of whether you do or don't.

[[Alvin]]: Jude, you...

[[Jude Mathis]]: Why'd it take me this long to figure out what Milla was trying to tell us?

[[Jude Mathis]]: Let's get moving, Alvin.*
