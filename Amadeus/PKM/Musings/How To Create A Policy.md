# How To Create A Policy

To get started navigate to the [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is IAM|IAM]] home page:

[](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/iamv2/home?region=us-east-1#/home)

1. Navigate to the service home page and tap on “[*Policies*](https://app.notion.com/p/4920c7bc48cf4041a4ba1c3bc7e482e0?pvs=21)”.

2. Tap on “*Create policy*”.
