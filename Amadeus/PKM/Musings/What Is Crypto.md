# What Is Crypto?

Cryptocurrency (also known as Crypto) is a type of [[What Is A Digital Currency|digital currency]] that relies on cryptography to secure its [[What Is Bookkeeping|transactions]]. Most of cryptocurrencies are known for their use of a [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]] (Hence the prefix "*crypto*”) to store [[What Is Bookkeeping|transactions]] on a [[Types Of Blockchains|public]] database.

The type of database used to store the transactions is not required to be a [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]]. It can vary depending on the cryptocurrency. [[What Is Bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and [[What Is Ethereum|Ethereum]] happen to be specific cases.

The [[What Is Distributed Consensus|leaderless consensus protocols]] used by [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchains]] of [[What Is Crypto|cryptocurrencies]] are designed to gradually decrease the production of the currency. This means the more [[What Is A Block|blocks]] are added to its [[What Is A Blockchain|blockchain]], the harder it will get to produce more units of the currency. This has sistemic repercusions such as having a finite amount of cryptocurrency available to be generated.

Cryptocurrencies appeared with the creation of [[What Is Bitcoin|Bitcoin]].
