# New Year, New Me

Woke up at 930AM today. I was feeling exhausted from yesterday's celebration however I wonder if what I am feeling is exhaustion from the previous year. I think 2022 was a really heavy year in terms of emotional baggage, it had lots of problems with my work and my family. Completing it meant we can leave all that behind.

I am feeling more excited this year than when 2022 began because I am seeing I will be progressing in my life in different ways:

- I will be moving by myself this month.
- I will be sharing apartment with Lourdes on the second half of the year (Hopefully).

It's weird to say that I'm not scared but rather excited. Wish I could have this mindset everytime.
