# Tackle Problems As They Emerge

The attitude displayed by Ban shows confidence however he doesn't necessarily feel that way. However the way he talks describe a mindset of “*tackling problems as they emerge*” or “*one problem at a time*”. I like this way of thinking as I prefer focusing on my immediate task and not overly concern myself with side-effects or the entire context as that may bring too much noise.

> *[[Katsuko Momose]]: You really wan to hear this?

[[Katsuko Momose]]: You’ll be stepping into some of the darkest corners of our society. Are you sure?

[[Katsuko Momose]]: You might get fired over this, Ban.

[[Ban Yasuji]]: All I care about is catching the NewGen Killer.

[[Ban Yasuji]]:  Well, I guess I’ll figure out what to do once I hear what you have to say*
