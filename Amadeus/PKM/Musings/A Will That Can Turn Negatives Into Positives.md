# A Will That Can Turn Negatives Into Positives

Referenced in a similar way in[[Overproduction Of Dopamine]]. Sena states how important is to have mental strength. This can be extrapolated to real life as negative thoughts are easy to come but hard to go. Only a strong will can withstand the challenges we face whether we want to face them or not.

> *[[Aoi Sena]]: “If you feel like you’re going to get more involved in this, whether you want to or not”

[[Aoi Sena]]: “You need to maintain a strong will, one that can turn negatives into positives.”

[[Aoi Sena]]: “Without that strength, your delusions will devour you.”*
