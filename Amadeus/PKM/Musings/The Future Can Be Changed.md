# The Future Can Be Changed

Wanted to keep record of this moment as I believe this is the most important lesson Okabe learned on his journey through the Beta attractor field. This is the moment Okabe is proven wrong by Daru about how the future can be changed and that there's still hope.

> *[[Suzuha Amane]]: You're lucky, Dad. You managed to deceive the whole world that way.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: I didn't have a choice!

…Deceived, huh?

I'd been deceived, too.

I'd been told I was fated to die in 2025.

And that even in the beta world line, that wouldn't change.

But no matter how it had happened, I was alive right now.

Even in the past, I'd been deceived by the future.

And It wasn't just me.

Suzuha and everyone else… the whole world had been deceived.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: The world… was deceived?*
