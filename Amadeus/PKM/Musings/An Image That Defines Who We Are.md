# An Image That Defines Who We Are

I captured this because I really like the idea of having an image that defines the core of our existence. I guess for me it is also sitting in a table with my family and Lourdes sharing together a meal.

> *They say that every person has a single image, somewhere in their mind that defines who they are.

For Hinae Arimura, that image was of her family, sated around a dinner table. Her mom, and dad, and her brother.

The four of them sitting around the table. That image, more than anything else, defined the kind of person that Hinae was.

Sometimes, they’d all go out somewhere for the day, then come back in the evening to eat at the dinner table. That was the rule at the Arimura household.

It was something so natural, and so normal, that she never doubted it.*
>
