# Your Worst Enemy Is What You Think You Know

This is a message similar to [[Doubt The World Know How It Operates|Doubt The World]]. This also resonates with the way [[Ban Yasuji]] thought about things

> *[[Katsuko Momose]]: You need to relax a little. Don’t take everything so seriously. I’m sure a lot of things are about to happen that will be beyond your understanding. When that happens, your worst enemy is what you think you know.*
