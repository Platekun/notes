# Trying Once or Twice Probably Won't Get You There

The path towards a goal is often challenging and filled with obstacles. Trying once or twice probably won't get us there. But I think starting there is the best way to get to it. I think that many futures are determined by our actions in the present.

> *[[Itaru Hashida]]: Release [Kurisu] from the bonds of Amadeus.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Huh?

[[Itaru Hashida]]: A message. From you, in the year 2025.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: A message from myself?

[[Itaru Hashida]]: The path to Steins Gate is a hard one. Trying again once or twice probably won't get you there.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: But I think starting there is the best way to get to it.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: I think that many futures are connected to the past.*
