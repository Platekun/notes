# Be Flexible In Your Thinking

This is a corollary from [[Your Worst Enemy Is What You Think You Know]] however this one emphasizes more on action rather that thinking.

> *[[Takeshi Shinjo]]**:** Are you Kunosato? Knowing Momose, I figured I'd be ready for anything, but I can't let a young girl into a crime scene-

[[Mio Kunosato]]: Don’t act normal when you’re surrounded by things that aren’t normal. Didn’t she tell you that would get in your way?

[[Takeshi Shinjo]]**:** Hey!

****She ignored Shinjo's words and walked right past him.*
