# Is What You're Seeing Truly Real?

I feel the idea of another higher dimension or world layer above us that control us make us insignificant somehow, a way to feel about it is feeling our struggles doesn't matter at all and that anything I do in my life will reach any significance. It is too easy to feel nihilistic about this topic and hence that's why I think people not try to question their existence in the grand scheme of things.

As a side note, it is a good insight the one that mentions that mixing truths in lies is a good way of constructing a believable lie. This hightlight the need to approach information and claims with a healthy degree of skepticism.

> *I looked over at the monitor.

My second character, Liselotte, was standing there, doing nothing.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Is what you're seeing truly real?

I remembered what Sena said. Over half of what she'd told me was true.

The one thing I hadn't been able to find a source for was that stuff controlling people's will, or their bodies. Maybe that was just Sena's delusion?

Mixing in a little litle with the truth was something that blackmailers and other people who wanted to make threats often did, because it worked.

I knew that, at least in my head, but after all the strange things that had happened to me over the past few weeks, I still couldn't help but feel scared…

I stared at Liselotte on the screen.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: Liselotte, is what you're seeing real?

I whispered, so that nobody around me could hear.

I'd thought about something similar a bunch of times.

Baselard, Liselotte's world, the world that I was looking at right now, was real to her, but to me it was fake.

Which meant that he person controlling me was seeing my world, and it was fake, too.

But just as Liselotte couldn't see her controller because he was in a higher dimension, I couldn't see the person controlling me, either.

Did Liselotte ever think about things like this?

She couldn't even move unless I told her to.

So, what about me?

Could I even get out of this reclining seat if the person controlling me didn't want me to?

I tried to stand up, just to see if I could. I succeeded easily.

Then I tried sitting down. I did it without difficulty.

Was what I just did…done of my own will?

Or was the person controlling me telling me what to do? I didn't have any way to tell.*
