# Create Standalone EBS Volume

![1. Navigate to the service home page and tap on “[*Volumes*](https://app.notion.com/p/9c4494fe67144f8e845597fa7e167be0?pvs=21)”.](How%20To%20Create%20A%20Launch%20Configuration%20Template/Untitled.png)

1. Navigate to the service home page and tap on “[*Volumes*](https://app.notion.com/p/9c4494fe67144f8e845597fa7e167be0?pvs=21)”.

![2. Tap on “*Create volume*”.](Create%20Standalone%20EBS%20Volume/Untitled.png)

2. Tap on “*Create volume*”.

![3. Choose the [volume type](https://app.notion.com/p/e83b583303a2433b88807fd84ecf06d3?pvs=21) and size.](Create%20Standalone%20EBS%20Volume/Untitled%201.png)

3. Choose the [volume type](https://app.notion.com/p/e83b583303a2433b88807fd84ecf06d3?pvs=21) and size.

![3. Choose the [availability zone](https://app.notion.com/p/2efeeacd41de4f95826074ead5533b30?pvs=21) and whether or not we are creating this volume from a [snapshot](https://app.notion.com/p/bf529b20934e40d2a5627c19636ebc67?pvs=21).](Create%20Standalone%20EBS%20Volume/Untitled%201.png)

3. Choose the [availability zone](https://app.notion.com/p/2efeeacd41de4f95826074ead5533b30?pvs=21) and whether or not we are creating this volume from a [snapshot](https://app.notion.com/p/bf529b20934e40d2a5627c19636ebc67?pvs=21).

![3. Optionally use check if encryption and tags are needed. Submit the form by tapping on “*Create volume*”.](Create%20Standalone%20EBS%20Volume/Untitled%201.png)

3. Optionally use check if encryption and tags are needed. Submit the form by tapping on “*Create volume*”.
