# Embrace Legacy, Keep Moving

> *[[Aoi Todo]]: Brother, A man as great as you shouldn't let yourself grow so small.

[[Aoi Todo]]: We are Jujutsu sorcerers, me, you, Kugisaki, Mr. Nanami. Our allies all of us are jujutsu sorcerers. so as long as we continue to live our dead comrades have not truly been defeated.

[[Aoi Todo]]: Sins and punishment is not the matter.

[[Aoi Todo]]: The moment we chose to live as jujutsu sorcerers our lives were no longer bound by such karma.

[[Aoi Todo]]: Attempting to find meaning in the deaths of the fallen can at times defile the memories of those we have lost.

[[Aoi Todo]]: But even so!

[[Aoi Todo]]: What have they entrusted you with?

[[Aoi Todo]]: There's no need to find your answer to that now.

[[Aoi Todo]]: But until you find it you cannot stop moving forward.

[[Aoi Todo]]: That is the one punishment imposed on those who live as jujutsu sorcerers.*
