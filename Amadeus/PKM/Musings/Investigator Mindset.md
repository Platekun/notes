# Investigator Mindset

I am still not sure why this conversation between [[Takeshi Shinjo]]  and [[Takuru Miyashiro]]  resonates within me. Is it because I also have an obsession with investigating things until I can declare my own truth? Is it because I despise people who lack the skill to search up and process information?

I can tell Shinjo admired ban and that he was very similar to [[Takuru Miyashiro]]  in that way of behaving regarding their investigations. I think I am somewhat similar too, even if I go against everyone I usually try to understand so I can feel in control of my reality.

After chatting a bit with ChatGPT I got to the conclusion that this "investigator mindset” have many characteristics that are common with what a software developer does. It also relates to the type of personality I have (INTJ) which means I could possibly have an investigator mindset.
