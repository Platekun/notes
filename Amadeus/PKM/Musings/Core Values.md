# Core Values

> *Yuji: **Stand up.** No matter how cruel the world becomes.

Sukuna: **Curse them.** Reject their ideals and hopes too.

Megumi: **Rescue.** Those from unjust destinies.

Yuta: **Fight.** For those you dearly love.

Gojo: **Lead.** Your strong, intelligent comrades.

Choso: **Protect.** On the bond between you and your brothers.

Nanami: **Achieve.** The duty you have sought to fulfill.

Maki: **Destroy.** The fate that binds you down.

Higuruma: **Judge.** For the sake of the justice that you carry.

Uraume: 𝘿𝙚𝙫𝙤𝙩𝙚. Yourself and your will to the King.

Hana: 𝙎𝙝𝙞𝙣𝙚. Upon your loved one sunk in darkness.

Hakari: 𝘽𝙚𝙩. You’re driven by the fever.

Takaba: 𝙇𝙖𝙪𝙜𝙝. Turn desperation into an opportunity.

Ino: 𝘾𝙝𝙖𝙡𝙡𝙚𝙣𝙜𝙚 𝙮𝙤𝙪𝙧𝙨𝙚𝙡𝙛. Carry on the will entrusted to you.

Kusakabe: 𝘾𝙤𝙣𝙣𝙚𝙘𝙩. Your beliefs to the new generation.

Todo: 𝙀𝙣𝙘𝙝𝙖𝙣𝙩. With the applause of your very soul.*
