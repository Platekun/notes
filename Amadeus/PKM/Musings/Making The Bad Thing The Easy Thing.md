# Making The Bad Thing The Easy Thing

One of [[Robert Martin]] advices always was that we should not marry to frameworks. Authors are delighted to be coupled to you but our domain should not depend on somebody else’s. We never now when we would want to migrate to something different. The perfect example to this is React’s practice of mixing UI with business logic.

> *The problem is that the path of least resistance in "frameworks" like React  is to put logic directly into components, or in framework-specific custom hooks that can't be easily tested.*
