# A Second Brail Reveal New Associations Between Ideas

A second brain serves as a laboratory where new ideas can appear by combining and associating existing ones. I really like the thought that just by storing things you increase the likelihood of finding patterns between them. Like it's something fated to happen eventually. [[Elizabeth Filips|Elizabeth]] did mention something about that when she tried to match things that only her could see in-between.

> *Neuroscientist Nancy C. Andreasen, in her extensive research on highly creative people including accomplished scientist, mathematicians, artists and writers, came to the conclusion that "Creative people are better at recognizing relationships, making associations and connections”.
By keeping diverse kind of material in one place, we facilitate this connectivity and increase the likelihood that we'll notice an unusual association.*
