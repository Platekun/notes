# Naive World View

Do we ever stop for a moment to think if what we are being show is the reality? Nowadays it's all about engagement and social media addiction, those short bite-size videos are clearly one way to prevent us from thinking if what we are seeing is truly real.

> *[[Aoi Sena]]: It must be so wonderful to believe in just the things you can see.*
