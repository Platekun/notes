# Unwavering Conviction

![Untitled](Unwavering%20Conviction/Untitled.png)

## Learned Lessons

- [We may as well keep walking forward our paths with our worries](https://app.notion.com/p/2667cba87bca43e1ad65669eecb7fee5?pvs=21), only willpower can strengthen our resolves (Rowen).
- [Justice is a complicated concept](https://app.notion.com/p/411944ec2b6647a19b79997425186f3e?pvs=21) because it means different things to different people. Sometimes, even if the same goal is shared, conflict is bound to happen (Gaius and Milla).
- The lack of a proper sense of drive in our lives can result into us following others without giving too much thought into our actions. However this is a fatal mistake, we cannot simply make others goals into our own. We need to think for ourselves and decide what we want to do with our life. ”*Isn’t that right, Milla?*” (Jude).
