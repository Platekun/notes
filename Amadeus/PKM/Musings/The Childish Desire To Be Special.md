# The Childish Desire To Be Special

Interestingly this talks about that one way of feeling special is the way we can “endure” different levels of hardships. Sounds like this is linked to what [[Suffering And Anger Are Necessary|Ayase explicitly said to him]].

This also reminds reminds me of the song [[Rashisa]], “*what does it mean to be yourself”*.

Takumi explicitly says that people who believe to be special are childish however this sentiment is the one that is mainstream. People use the excuse of “I'm not special” to start self-loathing themselves and trying to be proud they do not put effort at anything in life. W**hen there is no effort, there is no pride**.

> *On the monitor, Grimm's avatar had just level up. My KnightHeart was max level, so he wouldn't level up again.

As I rubbed my tired eyes, I wondered if it was time to make a third character after Liselotte.

I found myself thinking about experience points.

They were something you saw a lot in video games, but the way I saw it, there were experience points in the real world, too.

When I'd started ESO, I'd been a completely clueless newbie, but now I was Baselard's famous Lightning-Fast KnightHeart, the game's ultimate warrior.

So for instance…

Was it possible that some of the person inside me's experience points has been reset somehow?

Maybe that's why I'd forgotten things that I should've known. Maybe that's where the weird inconsistencies in my memories had come from.

Like with Shogun, or the demon girl.

Before I'd lost my experience points, I - that is, the person inside me - had used the nickname Shogun in chat.

Maybe the demon girl, Rimi, had been a big part of my life too, and I wasn't a friendless otaku.

But maybe before, I'd had some incredible power that would have let me fight the demon girl.

I wondered if I was the only person who could come up with an idea like this. Not that it really seemed that strange to me.

Humans could only ever judge things by standards they created for themselves.

Did that mean it was possible that I, and not just me, but everybody else in the world, was wrong about everything?

When I was little, I'd gone to the dentist, and they'd said to me "You did a great job, not crying when it hurts so much”.

back then, I'd felt kind of proud for enduring the pain, like I'd done something special.

But looking back, they were probably judging how much pain I was in by looking at how bad the cavity was.

But maybe the same level of pain was really bad for some people, and not so much for others.

Maybe the way people felt things or sensed things was totally different.

Take giving birth, for instance, I'd seen a lot of people online, for instance, saying that woman could handle the pain, but men couldn't.

Or what about marathon runners? Maybe what made them special wasn't their stamina, but the way they could endure pain.

Or maybe take S&M. Some people enjoyed receiving pain, and some people enjoyed giving it.

Maybe everybody had different was of experiencing their senses, and different levels that they could endure.

Maybe toothaches and headaches that I sometimes had to put up with were a level of pain beyond what an ordinary person could handle, and if I could pass that pain to them somehow, they'd die on the spot from the shock.

All this speculation left me with the selfish delusion that I was somehow special.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: So I'm special, huh? Nah…no way.

I denied it, but I'm sure it was a delusion everybody shared.

Every man wants to be the strongest in the world. Everybody wants to think they're special. Chosen.

They want to believe a dramatic future filled with adventure is awaiting for them, like on TV or in the movies.

The desire to be a hero, I guess you could call it. It was childish, really. Of course, I had that same desire.

Maybe it was my destiny in life to defeat the demon girl.

Of course, If someone actually told me to do it, I'd have several conditions.

I'd need cheat codes, obviously. Like God Mode, for one thing. Or invincibility. I didn't want to be scared or get hurt.

If I couldn't have that - in order words, If I had to do it right now, I would absolutely refuse.

Which meant that if the person inside me's experience points had been reset, I might be at a big disadvantage.*
