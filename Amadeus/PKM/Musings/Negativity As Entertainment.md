# Negativity As Entertainment

This is something I have been noticing in real life: Media in general like to talk about gruesome stuff on television and give it a lot of air time. People also like to talk about murders, robberies, and all bad stuff. They are all negative things and zero positivity. Worst part of this situation is that people keep talking about like it's some kind of game or gossip. I'm not sure the correct term for this is burnout however I do feel like we starting seeing life with a negative view thanks to the media consumption.

> *The news about the leaked video of the Group Dive quickly became a huge deal, of course. 

The net was, like Grimm said, in an uproar.

A lot of people on @chan were saying that this proved that all four NewGen murders were committed by the same person.

The TV news was just as excited.

>>140 was right. All the non-government-run TV stations, except for TV-TK, had done specials on it, and they’ve been showing the video nonstop.

None of the specials were loner than an hour, but the fact that the video was broadcast all over the country was shocking.

Of course, all the stations cut the last part where they actually jumped, but the effect they had was incredible.

The TV commentators were saying more or less the same thing as the internet, that all four of the NewGen cases were the work of the same killer.

It was inevitable that they’d say that. At last, the NewGen cases had something in common.

Whose eyes are those?*
