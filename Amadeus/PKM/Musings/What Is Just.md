# What Is Just?

> *[[Milla Maxwell]]: Justice is a complicated concept. It means different things to different people.

[[Gaius]]: And what is justice to you?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Heh, I thought this wasn’t a social gathering.

[[Gaius]]:  Answer me, Maxwell.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: It is the power of will that resides within one’s heart.

[[Gaius]]: Hmph. In this we are both in agreement. Those with this will power bear a responsibility to this world, the strong must protect and guide the weak.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Gaius, weakness isn’t an affliction of the body but of the heart. The world will always have weak willed people but that in itself isn’t a bad thing.

[[Gaius]]: Then we must protect the weak until they can become strong.

[[Jude Mathis]]: We?

[[Gaius]]: Yes, all who are strong must look up after the weak. It is the only way to ensure that future generations will have sufficient strength as well.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: So that's your solution to the problem I posed in Fezebel Marsh.

[[Gaius]]: As I told you then, I can chart a new future for mankind if I just have enough power. But if a man like Gilland takes the stage, a man who can only use power for his own gain, then mankind will be doomed to repeat the same mistakes.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: I see but I’m afraid It is not up to me to approve your answer. Your concept of justice is your own. It is not my place to interfere.

[[Gaius]]: Heh, as you say.

[[Jude Mathis]]: The two of you sure are alike.

[[Jude Mathis]]: No matter how extreme her words are, I feel there’s always truth to them. I get the same sense from you.*
