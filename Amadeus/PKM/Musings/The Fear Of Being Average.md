# The Fear Of Being Average

Robotic;Notes display one of my fears I cannot seem to get rid of. [[Akiho Senomiya]] is shown crumbling in tears after her sister [[Misaki Senomiya]] destroyed her robot.

In previous chapters [[Misaki Senomiya]]  said “*No matter what you do, you will be average*” to her sister before disappearing for 7 years.  [[Akiho Senomiya]] seems to have taken a lot of damage just from hearing her most important person in the world calling her average.

I always have this fear as well, that despite my struggles and progress I am still average. I wish to be better than others and I don’t think that’s a bad thing by itself. I think a virtue people should have is always striking to be better than the average. I like [[Akiho Senomiya]] ‘s attitude through the entire novel, always looking at a possibility to succeed regardless of the situation and trying to be better than her sister.

> *It’s all because of what she said to me before she left the island.

That no matter what I do, I’m just average.

I was always afraid…Afraid that from her point of view I was just a pathetic and annoying little sister.*
