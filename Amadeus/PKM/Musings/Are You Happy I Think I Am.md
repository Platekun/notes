# Are You Happy? I Think I Am

When doing some reflections this morning the realization that I may be happy with my life came up. I remembered how others talked about their life and their problems, or even they spend time on someone else's problems and not theirs. However when I looked at my own life I noticed that I am in a good spot, I'm in a decent job and I'm not trapped because I decided to stay here at my own choice. I think that all the last decisions I have made during my period of walmart were done without regret despite all things that came along with those decisions.

It is somewhat rare to feel calm at the same time that I'm rushing through a deadline for tomorrow. Where am I? I'm supposed to say “here”. This feeling of listing to Robotic;Notes's OST again reminds me of my times of depression and somehow feel calmed because it was a time where I felt saved.
