# What Is A Digital Currency?

A digital currency (also known as digital money) is a currency that can be stored, managed and exchanged electronically. The biggest difference from a traditional currency is the lack of a physical form of any kind which opens the possibility for [[What Is Bookkeeping|transactions]] to be done faster thanks to the systems they live in.

As other type of currencies, digital currencies can be used to buy and exchange physical goods and services however they are a technology in adoption.

Digital currencies need a “*computer system*” to work. This "*system*” may be the internet, a distributed database, centralized database, files or any kind of system that allows storage and exchange for this kind of assets.

Examples of digital currencies are: Bank transfers, debit and credit cards, [[What Is Crypto|cryptocurrencies]], E-wallets like Apple Pay and [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/), among many others.
