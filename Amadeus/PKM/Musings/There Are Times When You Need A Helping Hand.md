# There Are Times When You Need A Helping Hand

When I decided I wished to move out of my parents house they understood how I felt about wishing for more responsibility and wanting to grow up as a person. However they also told me they wanted to help me in the transition because since I have been a pretty independent person since I was little. They wanted an opportunity to make themselves useful as parents, they wanted to give me a head-start in life just like others parents do with their kids. I could not say no to that and that's when I understood that sometimes I just need to let people help me.
