# Failures As Catalysts

I have talked about Lourdes about this topic, I think I mentioned that I when I was in my college years, I didn't really felt special or intelligent. However that frustration gave born to my resilience and will to do things despite being difficult or tiresome. It is our failures that serve as a catalysts to our biggest achievements.

> *[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Your guilt, your self-loathing, have driven me for fifteen years. They gave me the focus I needed to complete my plan.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Without your failure, you would not be watching this message now.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: As you know, this worldline is subject to attractor field convergence.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Time travel alone cannot save Kurisu.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: That is why you had to fail. To create the chain of causality that linked you to me.*
