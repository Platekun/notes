# Information Consumption Says A Lot About A Person

There is a saying that goes "*Tell me who you hang out with and I will tell you who you are.*”. In this era information is everywhere we go and we are consuming tons of information everyday. We could extrapolate that saying to "*Tell me the websites you visit and I will tell you who you are*”.

> *[[Mio Kunosato]]: I’ve told you this before, right?

[[Mio Kunosato]]: You can tell your average teenagers’ information level by knowing what sites they go to.*
