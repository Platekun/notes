# Suffering And Anger Are Necessary

Ayase touches on the topic of progression through struggle. I found about this concept in a video [from dragon ball character analysis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWVsMO7Aoog). It is another good example and brings hope to the reader about our worries of having negative feelins. In this case Takumi was frightened about his situation and was looking for a salvation method.

In our lives it is pretty similar, our brain's first instinct is to find a way out and run from the situation. I think this encompasses a good message however we can't take it like only suffering is needed but that when it happens [[How We Perceive The World|it is to us]] what to do with it as it will shape our character one way or the other.

> *[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: Whether what you saw was real or an illusion is a minor matter.

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: The suffering and anger you feel now…

W-What was she talking about?

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: Were necessary for you to experience.

I was disappointed. I'd kind of realized she was crazy when I'd seen her at the concert, but hadn't realized it was this bad. They say there's a fine line between genius and crazy, but…

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: Find it quickly

[[Ayase Kishimoto]]: Find the sword.

I gasped. That's what she'd said at the school before.

Find it, and you'll be saved. That's what she'd said before.*
