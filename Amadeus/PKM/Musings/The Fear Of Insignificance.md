# The Fear Of Insignificance

![Untitled](The%20Fear%20Of%20Insignificance/Untitled.png)

## Learned Lessons

- The fear of insignificance can lead to a self destructive path. In this case, Takuru tried to show his talent by trying to stand out from the crowd by doing dangerous things (“[[I Am Different From Everyone Else|*You were scared to think that you’d be the kind of person who wouldn’t stand out.*]]”)[[I Am Different From Everyone Else|.]] Despite that, this fear is also why Takuru have really good detective skills among his peers. Fearing insignificance can make a person show their true potential.
- The importance of finding meaning in everyday life: While Takuru initially believes that he needs to do something extraordinary in order to feel significant, he eventually realizes that the meaning and purpose he craves can be found in everyday life. Takuru implicitly understood this when helping the school with their festival and when sharing time with his family. This is also why Serika's antagonized with Kurusu, as she was the one trying to make Takuru go straight and spend more time with his family / friends and not with the murders.
- We have to take responsibility of our acts and face repercussions, even if they were done with innocent / best intentions. Takuru without knowing passed his negative traits onto Serika when he created her. He only comes to that realization at the finale and it is why he decides to take all the repercussions of what she did, that's why he didn't want to accept the glory of being a hero (”*The case was perfect. It was just what I wanted to do. I thought I twas a great scoop! Any less and I wouldn’t have wanted to follow it until the end, right? But in the end … after I solved it, all that I’d be left with was emptiness, and the guilt from what I’d done”*).
