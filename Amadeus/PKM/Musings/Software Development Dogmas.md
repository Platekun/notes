# Software Development Dogmas

A dogma is a belief or set of beliefs that is accepted by the members of a group without being questioned or doubted. A real live example of this concept is how popular development practices rise in popularity with many developers being some kind of evangelists.

> *Any development practice that gets popular enough will eventually be turned into dogma.*
