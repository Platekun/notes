# What’s Wrong With Being Naive?

While I understood what [[Aoi Sena]]  said, what [[Takumi Nishijou]]  states here is clearly easy to sympathize with. This is a common problem I face where I know some of the things I should but not the entire picture and everyone around me either cannot help me or won't help me. However I don't want to be naive.

> *Ignorance is a sin. Some people say it’s bliss, but they’re naive.

That’s what Sena had said. But what’s wrong with being naive?

I wasn’t that strong. I was always looking for someone else to help me.

I couldn’t do anything on my own. I didn’t want to do anything.

I stood in the middle of the street and closed my eyes.

My biggest problem right now was that I knew some things, but now everything.

I knew about things like Shogun and the Di-Sword, things I didn’t even want to know. But if somebody asked me what they were, I couldn’t explain.

I didn’t have the answers.*
>
