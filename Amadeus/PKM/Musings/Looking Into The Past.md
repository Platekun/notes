# Looking Into The Past

> *Life was never meant to be redone.

And that's fine by fine.

Wouldn't you say so too, Kurisu?

I look up at the sky, and even though it's the middle of the day, I can see a single shining star.

Which suddenly reminds me.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Going to the past is possible right now. Look into the sky at night, and you can see how things were ten of thousands of years ago.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Every night, we casually travel through time.*
