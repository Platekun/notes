# Difficulty Subjective Terms

Using subjective terms that describe the difficulty of the topics we are explaining in our docs can lead to people feeling stupid just like [[Stupidity and Friendly APIs]].

> Avoiding the use of “simple”, “easy”, “basic” in a documentation can go a long way
