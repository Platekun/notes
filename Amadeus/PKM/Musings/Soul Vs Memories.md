# Soul Vs Memories

This is passage evokes some strange feelings as It made me think how does Reading Steiner is linked to a person's soul. I think it's because Okabe “*remembered*”. He never died however his mind was shattered and thankfully he had his memory restored. His “*soul*” never left his body, the key is about his memories. Okabe lived his life until 2025 and then he received his older memories.

If there is only one observer at the time, does that mean that observer equals the soul of Okabe?

I think this is somewhat similar to what Amnesia is, can people change because of Amnesia? Are they the same before and after amnesia? What if the time period between is really big and that causes a massive personality change?

What if we take this when we compare ourselves to a version of ourselves from years ago? I'm pretty sure we are the same person yet somewhat different. I guess memories is what differentiates us from our old selves.

> *[[Okabe Rintaro]]: These memories were once data, huh?

My memories since I was a kid.

My memories of that summer.

My memories with Kurisu.

All of them had been transformed into zeros and ones.

Could you still say that I was a person?

Could I still be called human?

[[Itaru Hashida]]: But it's the same as Makise-shi's time leap technology, right?

[[Itaru Hashida]]: You went through that a bunch of times, didn't you?

…He was right.

But even If I knew it in my head, it still felt strange.

I'd been cut off from 25 years of time.

It had all been forgotten and left behind in a digital world.

It felt like it had been stolen from me.*
