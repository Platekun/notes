# Remnants Of Death Vs Life Sensations

> *With everyone gone, the temperature in the lab quickly dropped. It was freezing.

But it was nothing compared to the cold I'd felt before.

That world I'd seen right after I'd erased Kurisu's memories from Kagari…

Kurisu was there, Mayuri was there… Everyone was there.

It was a scene from that very brief peace I'd had during that summer.

But that world had been so cold.

And now I knew why.

That was the world that I'd experienced when I was asleep, existing only as data.

A world built only of 0s and 1s.

Like being alive, even when you're dead.

or being forced to stay alive even though you were dead.

The people I loved were there.

But there was no warmth.

I could reach out and touch them…

But there wasn't even a little bit of warmth.

It was like floating in deep, dark space, without any light at all.

And Kurisu Makise was now experiencing that, too.

She was all alone in a cold world, without the slightest trace of warmth.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …

I raised my hand under the fluorescent light.

I could see the veins rising up out of it, and the red blood flowing in them.

I was alive.

There was warmth.

But could I really say that I was alive?

Now that my memories had been digitized…

After being exposed to that cold world, could I still really be called human?*
