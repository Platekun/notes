# How Envy Shadows

> *- Maybe I'm not that much better, though.

Maho silently laughed a little, and hated herself for doing it.

”Maybe I'm just as jealous as they are…” That feeling, which was a little like fear, was something that she always felt.

The girl in front of her was just that different from Maho.

Someone she could never reach, no matter how hard she tried.

Someone who'd surpassed everything she'd ever done almost instantly.*
