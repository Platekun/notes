# Everything Is Designed To Do Something

We don’t always know how *things* work however we something for sure: “***They are designed to work for us (humans)***”. That means figuring out how this *thing operates is not impossible*.

If we can figure out **what it is supposed to do**, then we can start making assumptions to guide us through the process.

> *[[Phi]]:* *Well, it was designed so that a human could operate it.

[[Phi]]: That means there's a logic to how it's set up. A computer computes, a calculator calculates, and an automobile mobiles.

[[Phi]]: Everything's designed to do something, and once you know what, you can start guessing how.

[[Phi]]: Once you start to figure out how to get it to do things, it's just a question of playing with it until it does the things you want it to do. See?*
