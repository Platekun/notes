# Coming To Terms With Your Decisions

> *[[Kurisu Makise]]: There's no way I can blame you, when I haven't time leaped even once.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …How can you be so understanding?

[[Kurisu Makise]]: I'm not. When you chose to save Mayuri with the Time Leap Machine, you must have known that no one would ever understand.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: So accept what you have done.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Do that, and you'll never forget the meaning of your choice.

So in other words, she's telling me to suffer on my own.*
