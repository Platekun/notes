# The Girl Named Senri Minamisawa

This fragment define exactly what Nono thought about her previous persona, Senri. this is also the reason why she insisted so much on Takuru going on about being special comapred to others and closing himself from interaction. From a perspective, she is kinda right, as she was also special like Takuru described himself however she didn't close to others, instead she was really popular (despite being an introvert).

It looks like there are two ways of going on about this then. Introvertness looks like it's not the defining factor in this matter.

> *I hated myself - the person named Senri Minamisawa.

I’d always been alone, ever since I was a kid.

At recess time in day care, everyone would go out to the playground and play on the swings and slide.

But I liked to stay indoors and read books.

No, I don’t think I actually liked it.

I just pretended I did so I wouldn’t have to interact with other people.

Even at a young age, I already thought that any interactions with others meant pain and sorrow.

That didn’t change in elementary school.

Instead of changing, my introverted personality got worse.

Now, after having lived as Nono Kurusu for years, I knew

If you try to stay in your shell, everyone will ignore you.

But you don’t have to do that. There’s so many fun things around you.

There’s so many friends you can enjoy them with.

I was always alone.

That didn’t mean I was bullied.

For as long as I can remember, I’d always been worried about what other people thought of me. I was good at avoiding becoming a target.

I tried my best not to stand out.

I tried my best not to make anyone mad.

That was my life.

But I still hated myself. I hated The girl named Senri Minamisawa.*
>
