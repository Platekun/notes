# What Are You Willing To Couple To Make Things Easier?

What am I willing to compromise for the sake of shipping things?” Many engineers would compromise coupling to implementations details if it were for the sake of speeding up the development process but that's a heavy loan to take.

> *I don't get what people mean by "testable" code. If you succeed at tricking a human to use your program, then surely you can trick a computer to use it, too.
But actually I do get what they mean: they want to give the computer shortcuts that aren't available to the human.*

*So it really comes down to: which other things are you willing to compromise to make your job of tricking the computer easier? For me, I'd rather design my program around all my other constraints, then figure out how to trick the computer (not that hard, honestly) after that.

The thing many engineers seem to be eager to compromise is coupling to implementation details. Maybe because I'm a framework implementor who changes those details all the time, I'm loath to ever make that trade.*
