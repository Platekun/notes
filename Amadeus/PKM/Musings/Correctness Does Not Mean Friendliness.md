# Correctness Does Not Mean Friendliness

At some point the React team decided to go the Functional Programming route with all its jargon, and while it is certainly a good framework it also becomes more nuanced because the terms they use to communicate with their community are not easily relatable.

> *I’m slightly worried that React started with descriptive names for things like `createClass` or `componentDidMount` - clear and understandable
Now it’s concurrent mode (I actually had to translate the word to Polish) and “algebraic effects”.*
