# Robotic;Notes

## Learnings

- Master your skills and always be prepared to show the world what you are made of when the time comes (Kai).
- Sometimes it is better to focus the inmediate problems (Akiho).
- People need to have dreams (long-term or short-term ones). Not having dreams can cause boredom and leads to [depression](https://app.notion.com/p/551a063d099e4a74a8744a6b8636b626?pvs=21).

- I really liked how Kai was portrayed as a one dimensional character at first with the repetitive line of “You will have to beat me at Kill Balad first” and evolves through the series in different moments like: Representing the robotic club on the robot fight tournament, how
- How Kai looks like a bad proitagonist but it’s an actual relatable protagonist.
- Kai depression about time stopping (Stopping caring about school, not sure what to do about the future).
- Robotic notes talks about dreams and what happens when you abbandon them: Subaru and Grandpa Fujita.
- Robotic Notes also talks about following someone else dream at starting your own path like Junna.
- Akiho’s inferiority complex against Misaki, how she was told that she is average.
- Robotic notes also displays how it feels living the impostor symdrome via Akiho. It displays her fear of being average and never catching up to her older sister.
- Robotic notes also showed me what is to be confident in your skills: Kona Furugoori and Kai are the best examples of this.
- I really liked Akiho as a character, she displays something I wished to apply more in real life: Believing that even if I don’t have the answers now I will find them in the way. Optimism.
- The determination Akiho showed Kimijima Ko when giving her last speech was chill inducing, not because it was not cheesy but because it showed determination of how big her dream is and that she was not going to let anybody crush her. This is the moment where Akiho understands she is not lesser than Misaki.
- I remember Kaito’s character a lot:
    - He showed to be dependable when he was needed the most: When he fought in representation of the robotic research club during the robotic tournament.
    - He tries to give people a push in their problems, not neccesarily solving them but just giving aid.
    - Kai suffers from his memories when Misaki was with them. She always belittled Kai and trashed him in everything he did with her. Kai devoted himself to videogames and became so good that common challenge was not challenging at all to him. He liked fighting good opponents like Nae or Misaki. Despite doing well in videogames he was not able to advance with his complex. It was when Misaki destroyed the Robot Expo that Kai came to a conclusion, that he will beat Misaki.
        - Character has skills but he is shadowed by a complex → Character comes to the resolution → Confidence + Skills do a good protagonist in real life.
- Robotic Notes showed me how stoicism is applied in everyday life context via: Kai and Akiho.
- My version of stoicism and how what Socrates said about possibilities was applied by Kai.
- My version of stoicism and how what Socrates said about the present was applied by Akiho.
