# I Won’t Depend On A God

> *[[Kurisu Makise]]: I wasn't-.

**Nakabachi**: You want my opinion? Fine, I'll give it to you.

**Nakabachi**: I'm going to publish it myself. End of discussion.

I won't court the mercy of a heartless god.

I will save Kurisu myself.

She won't die again.

Not at my hands.

Not at anyone's.*
