# Enjoying The Moment

These days I have been on the chip of preparing the project for future devs and have been rapidly creating abstractions over the sluggish boilerplate I have created around this year. The process involves removing bad abstractions, creating new ones over the boilerplate, cleaning up wrong imports, fixing some problems with tests and adding eslint rules for consistency. This has been a massive effort on my end however I sometimes wonder why I am doing this? Is it pride? Is it for the project? I kind of believe I am doing this for my self. Because I think I won't have the opportunity next time to do all of this. Possibly I will start in a project that already started so It won't be like this. I feel that even if it's not my property, I am somewhat of the intellectual author of somethings I wish to move somewhere and It will be incredibly helpful.
