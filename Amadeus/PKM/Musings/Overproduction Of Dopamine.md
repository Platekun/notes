# Overproduction Of Dopamine

I already touched on the topic about comparing the Di-Sword to a [[Suffering And Anger Are Necessary|method of salvation]]. However this pieces makes it feel more like the ability we have to gather strength and resilience when we face the chaos and challenges we face.

> *[[Aoi Sena]]: “Escalated biorythms cause an overproduction of dopamine in the neurons of the mesolimbic system. That's when the Di-Sword appears.”

[[Aoi Sena]]: “That can happen during dangerous situations, or when you're feeling excitement, sadness, hatred or laughter. It depends on the person.”

[[Aoi Sena]]: “The greater the amount of dopamine, the easier it becomes to observe the Di-Sword.”*
