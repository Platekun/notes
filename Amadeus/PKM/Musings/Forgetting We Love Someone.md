# Forgetting We Love Someone

![Untitled](Forgetting%20We%20Love%20Someone/Untitled.png)

- It is common for us to forget that we love someone explicitly. We can be very harsh in our communication with others and can hurt them despite loving them deep inside.
- The anger felt by Kevin shows how the blinding effect rage can cause our rational mind. It does not let us see more than our nose and feel what we feel is the correct feeling.
- [[There Are Times When You Need A Helping Hand|Everyone needs a helping hand sometimes]], you will find strangers (or even your neighbors) coming to your help when your family turns you down.
- It is clever how Kevin points out that being afraid won't help him in his situation and just decides he must face his fears either way.
