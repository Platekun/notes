# Echoes of Regret

> *[[Kurisu Makise]]: If you came from the beta word line, then you chose that world once before, doesn't it?

She was so far beyond what I could hope to be.?

In those brief moments she'd discovered all that.

She was right about everything.

But even so, I couldn't let her know.

Admitting thata would mean admitting that I'd left her to die.

There was no way I could do that.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …I'm sorry.

In the end, that was all I said.?

But that was an admission, all the same.

But instead of looking sad, she…

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Don't apologize, dummy. It was your choice, right? So have confidence in it.

She tried to cheer me up.

That's right.

Kurisu was right. I was the one who'd chosen the beta world lineq.

But -

[[Kurisu Makise]]: And you know, I don't think you were wrong.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: You just got here, so you wouldn't know, but for the last several months you've done nothing but blame yourself.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Not that you'd let me see it.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: But that ends today.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: It has to.

[[Kurisu Makise]]: Because if it doesn't, the guilt is going to keep eating away at you. And in the end, it will crush you.
