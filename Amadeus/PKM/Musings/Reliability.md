# Reliability

Availability is the ability of a system to operate continously without failure. Reliablity is often mentioned when speaking about system failures. A reliable system needs no repairs and should have an adequate performance.

The reliability of a system will be higher at its initial state of operation and can reduce over time (Exponential failure law).

This characteristic is found on [[What Is Cloud Computing|cloud-based]] systems. As customers we can trust their their service level agreements (also known as SLAs). In [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is AWS|AWS]]’ case, they are entirely responsible for managing the infrastructure.
