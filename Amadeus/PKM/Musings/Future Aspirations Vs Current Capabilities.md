# Future Aspirations Vs Current Capabilities

> *[[Alexis Leskinen]]: by the way, Lintahlo.

[[Alexis Leskinen]]: I know we discussed this before, but would you seriously consider being an exchange student?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Huh?

[[Alexis Leskinen]]: If it doesn't bother you, I'd be willing to write a recommendation letter.

[[Alexis Leskinen]]: Of course, you'd need to pass the test and the interviews.

I frantically straightened my posture.

Um… that's a very tempting offer, but…

I don't think I've got the academic talent for it yet.

My goal is to go to Viktor Chondria, and so I really don't want to screw it up.

And so I was planning on really buckling down and studying for at least another year.

Dr. Leskinen pursed his lips as I tried to stammer out an explanation.

But soon-

[[Alexis Leskinen]]: OK! OK!

He patted me on the shoulder and smiled.

I knew you were a very thoughtful young man.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: R-Really? No one's ever told me that before.

Saying it was one thing.

But maybe I was just a coward.

There were times when you needed the courage to move forward, without afraid of failure.

Was now one of those times?

[[Alexis Leskinen]]: So you'll try for it next year, then?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Yes, maybe…

Had I missed my chance?

Would Dr. Leskinen wait until next year?

I started to get nervous.*
