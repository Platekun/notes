# Artificial Miracles

Robotic;Notes’ protagonist mentioned this when arguing with Subaru (a classmate who refused to join her “Robot Club”) that they were going to win a tournament with an old robot from 10 years ago.

[[Akiho Senomiya]]'s attitude hits right on point with this and makes me wonder how many times I have spent doubting myself in some decisions. Decisions that could change my fate and maybe create a miracle that fixes my current situation.
