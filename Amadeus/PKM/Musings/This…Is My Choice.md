# This…Is My Choice

> *[[Okabe Rintaro]]: For I, the great Houoin Kyoma, will never lose to you, and I will never lose to fate!

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: I will find the path to Steins Gate!

This was the beginning of the long, long epigraph necessary to reach Steins Gate

- Madness is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting a different result.

Einstein had once said that, when he despaired of human folly.

But now I would gladly give myself over to that madness and folly.

I would use every bit of dedication and obsession within me to seek the lone different outcome, which the laws of God could not reach.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: That’s…is my choice!*
