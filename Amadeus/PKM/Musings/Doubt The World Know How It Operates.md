# Doubt The World. Know How It Operates

This piece of text touches the fascinating idea that humans run on electricity and states there is an hypothetical potential for sensory control.

The other topic this touches upon is about how much we rely on visual knowledge. It states that most of our information processing comes from the sight, this is an interesting fact as nowadays we are being bombarded with lots of information however we don't know what do with it.

I also like how she ask the question about our reality. Is what we are seeing truly real? This makes me think about how in software development we cannot take things for granted, we need to curious and understand how the world around us works.

> *Do not take things for granted. Be curious, doubt the world. Ask yourself questions regarding something. Naivety can become a sin if prolonged.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Where was the error?

Sena asked me another strange question, completely ignoring my confusion.

[[Aoi Sena]]: that's why you followed me, right?

I shook my head quickly in confusion, and her expression only grew sterner.

Finally, she looked away from me, sighing a little and nibbling on her popsicle.

When she released me from her terrible gaze, I almost collapsed.

I wanted to sit on a seat, but she'd probably say something. I gave up on the idea.

[[Aoi Sena]]: You saw the error I created, right?

[[Takuru Miyashiro]]: What do you mean, error?

If that was what she meant, then yes. I'd seen it. I'd followed that strange white chain all the way here.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: B-But it's gone. It disappeared.

[[Aoi Sena]]: I got rid of it. It was my delusion, after all.

Delusion what was she talking about?

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: T-Then what about the sword?

[[Aoi Sena]]: You have one too, right?

Huh? she thought I had one? Of course I didn't! This conversation wasn't making any sense.

[[Aoi Sena]]: More importantly…

[[Aoi Sena]]: Who are you?

[[Aoi Sena]]: you seem to be able to “see.”

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: What?

Sena glanced at me before jerking her head in the direction of the window.

[[Aoi Sena]]: What do you see outside the window?

Outside the window?

People, I saw lots of people

There were ven more than there'd been a second ago.

Just looking at them made my head hurt.

People with their heads down playing with their cellphones, people laughing as they talked on the phone, people just standing and watching other people go by…most of the people I could see from where I was standing seemed to be waiting for someone else.

Actually, how come none of those people actually tried to come inside the train?

[[Aoi Sena]]: Do you think there's a perfect, complete world out there?

[[Aoi Sena]]: That's imposible. there are errors. They exist.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Because everything… humans included… run on electricity.

Was this her weird, personal fan fiction?

I mean, I had similar fantasies all the time.

I wasn't going to tell her not to have them.

All the novels, all the movies, all the anime in the world came from goofy fantasies.

So why were fantasies of a novelist or a director okay, while the ones amateurs like me came up with were mocked?

and what's more, think about it… All the great people of history had goofy, childish fantasies.

The Wright brothers honestly believed they could fly over a hundred years ago.

Napoleon actually told people, "The world 'impossible’ is not in my dictionary”.

You shouldn't make fun of somebody for having fantasies. All of my history's greats had fantasies.

The problem was… this girl had suddently started sharing hers with me.

[[Aoi Sena]]: What you see doesn't matter. What matters is what you are being shown.

[[Aoi Sena]]: People receive information about the outside world from their senses. Over 80% of that information comes from vision.

[[Aoi Sena]]: The images your vision shows you are transmitted by optic nerves to the brain in the form of electrical pulses.

It was kind of like a phone, I though.

A phone took somebody's voice and turned it into an electrical signal, and then sent that information to someone else far away.

[[Aoi Sena]]: A phone, huh? It's similar, in a way.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: …

She read my mind!

No that was impossible. Had I spoken aloud without realizing it again?

[[Aoi Sena]]: Do you know about VR technology?

Virtual Reality?

Of course, this time I was careful not to say it aloud.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Visual Rebuilding.

Hugh? I was wrong. I'd never heard of that.

[[Aoi Sena]]: It's a technology being researched by American psychophysiologists.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Take someone who's been blind from birth, and make it so they can see what's in front of them. It uses a tool similar to a video camera, you see.

[[Aoi Sena]]: You're wondering how that's possible, but the principle is quite simple.

[[Aoi Sena]]: The video recorded by a camera and a human nerve impulse are both, at their most fundamental level, electrical signals.

[[Aoi Sena]]: All you need is something to convert the video into nerve impulses.

[[Aoi Sena]]: They've already succeeded in tests. One research team already has a patent.

A patent…Was that true?

It felt like a really well-thought-out delusion, but what kind of delusion includes stuff about patents?

[[Aoi Sena]]: If you read the patent carefully, you'll discover something.

Sena turned her gaze away from the window and toward me.

She crossed her legs again, slowly.

If it were possible to convert a certain deliberately-chosen information pattern into nerve impulses…

You would be able to control all five of a person's senses, and in fact, their will itself.

[[Takumi Nishijou]]: …

The train was right in front of Shibuya Station, but it was strangely quiet. It felt like the sound was being shut out.

Of course, it was just my imagination… but I remembered the empty Shibuya I'd seen days ago, and couldn't take my eyes off the crowd outside.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Like I said before, people run on electricity.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Electricity travels not just through the brain, but all the nerves in the body.

[[Aoi Sena]]: In other words, you could control their movements as well. Theoretically, that is.

This girl…

I was in shock.

She was more delusional than even Yua or me.

What she said sounded logical on the surface, kind of, but it was clearly just a fantasy.

[[Aoi Sena]]: In fact, the team that got the patent in shifting their research in that direction.

[[Aoi Sena]]: The concept is so dangerous that the American president issued an executive order to try to stop it.

How much of what she was saying was actually true?

[[Aoi Sena]]: The order banned any organization from researching the secrets of the human body without prior approval from the government. The same goes for providing any such organization with support.

Sena looked down. She tightly clenched her fists as they rested in her lap.

But her world is full of scum.

The kind of scum who'll ignore ethics of any kind, and think nothing of sacrificing others for their own gain.

It makes me want to throw up.

She put the last of melting Garugari-kun popsicle into her mouth, then stood up.

I stepped back, only to find my back against the wall.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Ignorance is a sin. There are people who say, “There are some things you’’ll be happier not knowing”. but that’s simply naive.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Doubt the world. Learn how it works.

[[Aoi Sena]]: This world isn't perfect.

She was walking toward me.

I though she might punch me, and tried to shield myself with my arms. But Sena just walked straight past me.

[[Aoi Sena]]: Is what you're seeing truly real?

She whispered as she passed me, without so much as glance my way.

And then she left the train.

I watched Sena walk away through the train car's window, standing there in shock. She disappeared into the crowd without turning once.

In the end, I never did figure out what she was trying to say.*
