# We All Depend On Someone's Kindness At Some Point In Our Lives

It is important to remember that sometime, somehow someone will depend on your kindness. The same way we are going to depend on someone else’s.

> *[[Yuugo Tennouji]]: She sold it to me for cheap. It's thanks to her the Braun Tube Workshop exists.

[[Yuugo Tennouji]]: You could say she saved my life.

[[Yuugo Tennouji]]: Once, I asked her why she treated me so well. It wasn't like we were related or anything.

[[Yuugo Tennouji]]: But she just smiled and said…

[[Yuugo Tennouji]]: “We all depend on someone's kindness at some point in our lives. When the time comes, you pass the gift along.”*
