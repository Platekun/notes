# Despite Everything…

![IMG_7229.jpeg](Despite%20Everything%E2%80%A6/IMG_7229.jpeg)

It’s the beggining of a new year. A new year with higher expectations, new hopes and new dreams.

2024 was one bittersweet year, we got to fulfill our biggest dream of travelling to Europe however the biggest slide of the year was at the end when my relationship started breaking down.

We managed to solve our differences for now, enough so we could start anew but we don’t know what the future holds.

I still don’t feel comfortable woth her, my family or even myself.

I brought sadness to my family, I abandoned my father in his biggest life challenge, I failed to be a good boyfriend and I failed to make everyone happy.

Looking at the mirror I see my reflection, a kid full of hopes and carefree. Despite everything that has happened, I’m still me.
