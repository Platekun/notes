# Information Forgery

I really like how Kunosato explains to a non-expert person how the information we found on internet can be forged by someone else who is trying to manipulate us into thinking or acting in certain ways. This is why I dislike multiple of the click-bait articles or articles that are written with lots of hype.

> *[[Mio Kunosato]] **:** Do you know what this year’s study said about media usage rates?

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** Seventy percent of people get their news from the internet. For teenagers, it’s 80%. For. Those in their twenties and thirties, it’s 90%. In all cases, it’s a higher percentage than gets their news from TV.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** If you limit yourself to urban areas only, the percentages are even higher.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** Less than 10% of people in their teens and twenties read a daily newspaper. That figure hasn’t changed much in the ast two years.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** Over half of middle-school students have a smart phone. Ninety-five percent of high school kids do. And if you have a smartphone, you are using the internet.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** How many people do you think rely solely on the internet for their information?

[[Takeshi Shinjo]] : Wait, what are you talking about?

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** How many people do you think rely solely on the internet for their information?

Kunosato suddenly stood up and brough over the mobile PC she’d been using to stream. She opened up something on the screen for Shinjo to see.

It was the home page of a major newspaper, dated yesterday.

There was an article about a Shibuya resident suing Koshin for invasion of privacy.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** I caused this.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** One of the members of a group opposed to the surveillance society is a tweeter addict.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** I made a bunch of similar accounts and home pages. Each of which looked like it was created by a different person.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** Then I pointed them to favorable juidicial precedents to make it look like a bunch of people were telling him we was a victim.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** Since he still seemed reluctant, over the weeked I recorded some rude behavior by someone at Koshin and got the whole internet mad about it. And the result “- was this”, she said as she tapped the PC. 

[[Takeshi Shinjo]]**:** No way…

**Mio Kunosato:** When a person relies on the internet for their information, they’ll accept any offline source that confirms their natural biases.

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** I’d be willing to bet that he nevers read anything about the relevant case law, besides what I pointed out to him.

”Not that I've ever seen his face”, Kunosato continued. The way she spoke alone was enough to make Shinjo believe she was telling the truth.

[[Takeshi Shinjo]]**:** W-Why would you do something like that?

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** Momose told you that Koshin probably up to something, right? I agree. So I’m trying to damage their public image as much as possible. And-

[[Mio Kunosato]]**:** What I want now is information on the Sumo Stickers.

The screen was indeed showing a picture of the Sumo Sticker.

And then…

[[Takeshi Shinjo]]**:** (The Hekiho Academy Newspaper Club?)

Shinjo's gaze landed on a phrase in the video's introduction.

Wasn't that where those kids who snuck into the love hotel went?
