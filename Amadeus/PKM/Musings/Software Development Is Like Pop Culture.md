# Software Development Is Like Pop Culture

I saw this and immediaty thought that it is how Twitter works. It is about participation and putting your name out there however most people hate older development practices like they are a plague, despite the fact they stand on their shoulders.

> *Computing is pop culture. Pop culture holds a disdain for history. Pop culture is all about identity and feeling like you're participating. It has nothing to do with cooperation, the past or the future - it's living the present. I think the same is true of most people who write code for money. They have no idea where [their culture came from].*
>
