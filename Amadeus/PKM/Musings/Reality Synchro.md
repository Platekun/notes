# Reality Synchro

I could say that when she speaks in plural is as a way of referring to the idea that it is a common sentiment. Sometimes common sentiments are shared in environments like families where you could feel an aura around those people involved. [[Rimi Sakihata]]  at the end wants to state that despite that everyone around is gloomy, we should try to be optimistic about our journey and not let others drag us down with their negativity.

This piece is a different way os stating what [[A Will That Can Turn Negatives Into Positives|turning negatives into positives]] is about.

> *[[Rimi Sakihata]]: The winter sky seems like its colors have faded.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: Why do you think that is?

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: It must be because it’s cold, and everybody’s looking at the ground.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: So, there aren’t as many people who want it to be blue.

[[Rimi Sakihata]]: So even if it’s cold, let’s look up as we walk.*
