# Complexity Subjectiveness

Everybody says “*Write simple code*”, “*YAGNI*”, etc.  Everybody tries to follow the [‣](https://app.notion.com/p/ad26f03041684268985bb9c38e766313?pvs=21).

Despite that it is really difficult to describe when something is “*complex*” vs something “*simple*”. There is no strict definition to something being “*complex*” because the concept is subjective and depends on the point of view of the other person. And usually when people throw the term “*complex*” it is often referred as something bad.

Can an objective spectrum or scale be created for complexity?

> *Everybody says "Write simple code". Everybody says "complexity is bad". But we don't have a "concept" of complexity. We can't "do" anything with it besides say it's bad. There's only two popular treatments of complexity as a concept, and neither goes deeper than a basic level.*
