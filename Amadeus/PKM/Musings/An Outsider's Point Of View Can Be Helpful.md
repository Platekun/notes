# An Outsider's Point Of View Can Be Helpful

Thinking this way proves that she knows how to solve problems. Sometimes it is just easier to ask for other's point of view. Maybe they can see something so obvious we can't.

> *[[Katsuko Momose]]**:** One of my kids knows more about this than I do. And they say the want to see the crime scene personally. Can you help them out?

**[[Takeshi Shinjo]] :** You want me to let an outsider into a crime scene?
*****
*[[Katsuko Momose]]:* *It’s always outsiders who will give you the hints you need to solve the case*
