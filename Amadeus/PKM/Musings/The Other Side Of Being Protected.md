# The Other Side Of Being Protected

[[Takuru Miyashiro]] gave [[Serika Onoe]] the shape of someone who is weaker than him (in some sense), so he can protect her. However this also means [[Serika Onoe]] was suffering as she was limited to being lesser than [[Takuru Miyashiro]].

This contradicts what is established on [[The Desire To Protect]].
