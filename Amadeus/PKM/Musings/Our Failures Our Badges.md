# Our Failures Our Badges

This character is trash at the beginning on the novel but can throw one or two wisdom pieces. I agree with him on this one, most of times you need to do things by yourself. He mentioned this because Akiho Senomiya expected to receive more help from others.

A similar thought is shared by [[Oshino Meme]] in [[Only We Can Save Ourselves]].

> *[[Mitsuhiko Nagafukada]]*: *There’s a certain saying. I really like it, so I want you to remember it well. Relying on others leads to no good.

[[Mitsuhiko Nagafukada]]*: *Do everything you can on your own, even if you make mistakes. You can wear those failures as badges later.*
