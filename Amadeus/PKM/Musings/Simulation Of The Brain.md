# Simulation Of The Brain

> *But she did have one theory.

Amadeus was a simulation of the human brain.

Theoretically, there were no differences between it and the real thing.

As proof, it was possible to take memory data from one person and deploy it to Amadeus, making it behave like a copy of that person.

This meant that anything that could happen to a human brain could happen to Amadeus.*
