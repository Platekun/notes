# Scroll Restoration And Custom Back Buttons

Scroll restoration is not about restoring it to the top of the previous page but rather going back to the place before navigation.

> *The back button not going back to the same place you were before. In apps back can jump you back three or more nav actions, approaches vary a lot. Browser back can also do this when js apps are modal heavy but most frustrating for me is back going to the top of the previous page*
