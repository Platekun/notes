# Hippocampal Gyrus

> *The hippocampal gyrus is found on the outside edge of the hippocampus. It is the part of the brain that controls memories and spatial recognition. It is responsible for spatial processing, as well as the sensation of being “familiar” with a situation or place. It plays an important role in memory encoding and retrieval.*
