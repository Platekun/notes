# It Doesn't Matter If I Stand A Chance Or Not

After having a night call with Lourdes, I noticed that maybe I could start working with Thresholds earlier than expected.That thought made me anxious during the night.

When I woke up today I decided to listen to motivational videos I liked. After my workout, I'm noticing I'm not feeling fear…I guess that is a good start. Nonetheless, I have a mix of emotions of leaving Walmart and joining a small team: “*I want to keep my job*”, “*I want to give a good impression with my new team*”, “*I want to show everybody I can with anything*”.

I liked a lot one phrase from my workout routine:"*It doesn't matter If I stand a chance or not. I have to fight you here and now*”.

![Untitled](It%20Doesn%27t%20Matter%20If%20I%20Stand%20A%20Chance%20Or%20Not/Untitled.png)

I need to trust the process, my process. As long as I have a system that helps me prepare myself I can overcome things eventually. That is my truth.
