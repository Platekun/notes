# Universal Bonds: Understanding Each Other

Found this when reading an epilogue for Steins;Gate. Despite not being the most romantic person, [[Kurisu Makise]] says something really profound about the lives of the people in a relationship. They live different lives which can be on different times however there is a universal bond that lets them connect and understand each other.

> *[[Kurisu Makise]]: Even if we are walking different lives at different times… we share something fundamental and can understand each other. Something, for sure.*
