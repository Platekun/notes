# Learning to Adapt in This Chaotic World

- **Seeking Solutions Outside of Belief Systems:** Depending on the challenges we sometimes will need to seek solutions outside of our beliefs system.
- **Understanding the Universality of Negative Feelings:** Nobody is special for having negative feelings, everybody has them. What is important is what we do with them. They can change how we perceive the world.
- **Developing Resilience and a Strong Will:** Our life is a constant stream of challenges, for that sole reason we need to have strong will that can turn negatives into positives.
