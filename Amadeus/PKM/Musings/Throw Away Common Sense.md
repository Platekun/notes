# Throw Away Common Sense

I like the phrase, it captures the idea that sometimes in order to find something out of the extraordinary we must throw common sense and see things clearly as they are. Momose also mentioned something similar. When facing confusion, sometimes [[Your Worst Enemy Is What You Think You Know|your worst enemy is what you think you know]].

> ”[[Shogun]]*”: Throw away common sense and simply see it as it is.*
