# Unconventional Thinking

This is a lesson that from [[Ban Yasuji]]  to [[Takeshi Shinjo]] . I don't think we need to take literal word of being "*crazy*” but rather "*unexpectedly*” or "*unconventional*”. Sometimes acting in unexpected ways may be the key to finding a solution to a problem, I suppose it makes sense on problems that other people have tackled already in common and expected ways and found no solution that way.

> *Shinjo cut her off before she could continue her lecture and gave her a description of the scene. This was a clear violation of the rules, but the only way to solve a crazy case is to be a little crazy yourself.

That was the first thing he'd learned from that [[Ban Yasuji|older detective]].*
