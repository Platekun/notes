# Mental Clarity Is a Sign Of Braveness

> *[[Alvin]]: You gonna stick around?

[[Jude Mathis]]: Yeah.

[[Alvin]]: Okay. I'll be back at the village.

[[Jude Mathis]]: I have to figure out what I can do.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Oh, what's wrong? I thought you were going to rest.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: I would say the same to you. You didn't go back to the village?

[[Jude Mathis]]: No.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Well then, let's go set you up with the villagers.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: What's wrong? Worried you won't fit in?

[[Jude Mathis]]: No, that's not it.

[[Jude Mathis]]: So, what are you going to do now?

[[Jude Mathis]]: Will you return to Fennmont to destroy the Lance of Kresnik?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Yes.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: When you consider that the Lance drained mana from the Four, along with the other people there...

[[Milla Maxwell]]: We can assume the mana serves as some kind of fuel. The Lance must gather mana before it can be used as a weapon.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: I doubt it will be ready immediately. I suspect their mana-gathering activities will continue.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Are you planning to go alone?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Stop beating around the bush. If you want to tell me something, just say it.

[[Jude Mathis]]: I want to know, how come you're so brave?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: You've taken an interest in me, haven't you?

[[Jude Mathis]]: ......

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Bravery. That's not really it.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: I have a task to perform.

**Milla**: And I do whatever it takes to complete the task. It's that simple.

[[Jude Mathis]]: But you're only human now. Isn't this mission too big for just one person?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: I mean, you could die!

[[Milla Maxwell]]: That changes nothing. The task must be completed.

[[Jude Mathis]]: You really are brave.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Any other questions? Then let's go back.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Hey!

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Yes?

[[Jude Mathis]]: Can I come, too? With you?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Your whole life was turned upside down because you got involved with me.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: You don't regret it?

[[Jude Mathis]]: Well, sure... A little bit.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: But it's too late to cry about it now. It's not like I can turn back time.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Now that I'm neck-deep in this, I want to help you, if I can.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: You really are a do-gooder, aren't you?

[[Jude Mathis]]: Y-You think so?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: I didn't stay behind in the shrine to rest; I was hoping to sneak off without dragging you into things again.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Really?

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Yes. Consideration for others was something I learned on our little journey together.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: It's not as easy as I thought.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Anyway, let's head to the village.

[[Milla Maxwell]]: Now that you found me, I suppose I'm in no rush to leave anymore.

[[Jude Mathis]]: Okay.*
>
