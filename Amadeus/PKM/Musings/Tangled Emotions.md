# Tangled Emotions

My weekend is still kind of unplanned. My fridge hasn’t been repaired yet as I’m waiting for the technical service to come, so we made some kind of Mexican lasagna with Lourdes with some can ingredients we had. I feel she got to participate more this time and she seemed more happy. I usually don’t let her cook and take over her for all the chores as this always triggers some kind of trauma around marriage and the couple life (She believe I’m going to make her do all the chores which is exactly why I don’t let her do them).

Got back from the holy week, we can call that a vacation. However the time I spent until Wednesday was a peaceful one despite being in work. On Wednesday my anxiety increased as my client started showing that I needed to start working on a new set of journeys for the application. Started off anxious but I think I’ll be done by next week (at least the most part) so I have a pretty optimistic view on this.

On the afternoon of the same day, Lourdes got too drunk from wine and she decided to get some sleep, which left me with some extra “me time”. On the evening we agreed on going to the mall as we decided to go to a horror movie with her family (it seems to be a common liking among them). We spent some time window shopping here and there while it was due the time for the movie.

Lourdes touched again on the topic of Nancy, of course she always does this jokingly and I joked back (The recurring theme in our relationship is that she is other girlfriend). This topic or joke always makes me somewhat confused about our common friend. Nancy is an attractive woman which I I have some things in common. Lourdes has even asked what would I do if “she confessed she liked me” (I told her that I’d just answer that I couldn’t reciprocate the feelings tho). Am I attracted to her? I feel that it feels like Lourdes believes I’m more desirable that what I believe I am. Lourdes mentioned that one time Nancy told her how she wanted someone like me as a couple. I just told Lourdes that she should take that as a compliment if that’s the case as she took the prize. I’m not sure I want to jump on a relationship with Nancy but mixed feelings reside in my stomach (or heart?).
