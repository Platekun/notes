# What Is ELB?

The Elastic Load Balancing (also known as ELB) service is an service offered by [[Amadeus/PKM/Musings/What Is AWS|AWS]] used to distribute traffic across resources while making sure they are utilized evenly, in other words, [[Load Balancing|load balancing]].
