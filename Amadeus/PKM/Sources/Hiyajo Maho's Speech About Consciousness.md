# Hiyajo Maho's Speech About Consciousness

> *[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Have you ever heard of a philosophical zombie?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: It's… a thought experiment, right? Something that looks and behaves just like a human, but lacks consciousness and emotions… And whether or not you could objectively distinguish it from a human.

I hesitated a little as I answered her sudden question.

She nodded, satisfied, like a teacher would do.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Then let's say that right here there was an AI that looked just like a human.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: It looks like a human, and acts just like one, too.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: When someone's in trouble, it reaches out to help them, and when it's sad, it cries.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Maybe those are just programmed processes, but humans are no different. Everyone thinks and reacts in accordance with their own individual processes.

I thought I understood what Maho was trying to say.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: In other words, AIs have feelings, too?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: At least, we scientists are doing our best to create them.

She puffed out her chest with pride.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: So, Okabe, Do you think you're not an AI with emotions?

If they looked just like a person, and thought just like them, and had feelings too…

They were a person.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: No…

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Right? It's memories that makes someone who they are, anyway.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Memories are everything people have experienced since they were born.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: So, Okabe Rintaro. Even if your memories were once data, you've got your body and your memories. You're human. You're Okabe Rintaro. Don't worry about it.

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Hiyajo…

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: And anyway, doesn't Kurisu's time leap machine digitize data to send it back in time?

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: So is there any need to worry about this now?

That was what Daru had told me in the future, too.

But strangely, when she said it, it made me feel better.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Maybe Kurisu would've been able to come up with a better way to put it, but…

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: No, that's not true. This was more than enough.

Maho had given me her opinion, as a brain scientist, that I was still me.

There was a strength in her words, just like there had been in Kurisu's when she'd driven me forward.

I remembered what Maho had said before -

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: You're a genius, and in the end, I'm just Salieri.

She'd been speaking to the memories of Kurisu inside Kagari.

But - 

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: Hiyajo, you're not Salieri.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: Huh?

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: You're Amadeus.

[[Hiyajo Maho]]: …

That's right.

I'm here, right now.

I'm alive, and here in this time, not in that cold sea of black data.

I'm here in this time.

A light came on in the darkness.

My cold heart began to warm.

But -

[[Okabe Rintaro]]: …

Kurisu - she was still trapped inside that world.

Inside that sterile, lifeless world that froze you to the depts of your soul.

Even after her death, her memories were still alive.

And there were all kinds of conspiracies forming around her living memories.

Even after she lost her body. Kurisu's pain continued.

No, it wasn't just Kurisu.

Kagari, and Suzuha.

Everyone was suffering, everyone was miserable, but still they tried to move forward.

I thought that even if the Third World War came, no matter what happened to the world, as long as Mayuri was happy, that was all that mattered.

I tried to think that.

I thought that no matter how much blood and tears awaited us in the future, as long as things were fine now, that was all that mattered.

I thought I'd understood.

I thought I'd understood how awful the future that Suzuha described was.

And how hard Daru and suzuha would have to fight. I thought I understood all that.

Suzuha, firing her gun and staining her hands with blood like it was nothing at all.

Mayuri and Faris, pale and colorless.

Lukako lying on the ground and never getting up again.

Kagari and the others, whose lives were ruined by fate.

I'd seen them with my own eyes and touched them with my own hands, and then I finally understood.

I'd been only pretending to know, so I could run away.

I'd told myself that I was going to die in 2025, and looked the other way.

2025 -

I wasn't dead.

Everyone just thought so.

Everyone had been deceived.

That's right, deceived.

Me in the past,

and everyone else in the future.

The world - was deceived.

I thought the future was settled.

But a different future awaited me.

[[Itaru Hashida]]: But I think starting there is the best way to get to it. That's what you said 11 years ago.
