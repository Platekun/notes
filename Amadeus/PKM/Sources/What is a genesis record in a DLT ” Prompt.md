# "What is a genesis record in a DLT?” Prompt

> *In a distributed ledger technology (DLT), a genesis record is the first record in the ledger. It is the starting point for the entire DLT and serves as the foundation for all subsequent records.

In a blockchain, the genesis record is the first block in the chain of blocks. It is a special block that does not contain any transactions and is used to initialize the blockchain. The genesis block typically includes a message or other information that is included by the creator of the blockchain.

In a DLT that uses a different technology, such as a hashgraph or a directed acyclic graph (DAG), the genesis record may have a different form. For example, it may be a special node in the DAG that serves as the starting point for the DLT.

Overall, the genesis record is an important part of a DLT because it marks the beginning of the ledger and serves as the foundation for all subsequent records. It is typically created by the creator of the DLT and is used to initialize the system.*
