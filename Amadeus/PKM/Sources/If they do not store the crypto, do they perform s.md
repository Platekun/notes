# "If they do not store the crypto, do they perform some kind of read through the blockchain to return a balance?” Prompt

> *Correct! Cryptocurrency wallets do not actually store your cryptocurrency. Instead, they store your private and public keys, which are used to access your cryptocurrency on the blockchain

To check your balance, the wallet will use your public key to search the blockchain for any transactions associated with your wallet. It will then calculate your balance by adding up all the transactions that are associated with your wallet, taking into account any transactions that have been spent

In this way, the wallet can provide you with an up-to-date balance of your cryptocurrency by reading the blockchain and performing the necessary calculations.*
