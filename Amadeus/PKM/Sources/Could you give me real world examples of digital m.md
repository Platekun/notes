# "Could you give me real world examples of digital money?” Prompt

> *Digital money refers to money that is transferred electronically, rather than physically. Some examples of digital money include:

1. Cryptocurrencies: These are decentralized digital currencies that use cryptography for security and are not backed by a central authority. Examples of cryptocurrencies include Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Litecoin.

2. E-wallets: These are digital wallets that allow users to store, send, and receive digital money. Examples of e-wallets include Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.

3. Mobile money: This refers to the use of mobile phones to transfer money and make payments. In countries where traditional banking infrastructure is limited, mobile money has become a popular way to transfer money and make payments. Examples of mobile money services include M-Pesa in Kenya and Alipay in China.

4. Bank transfers: Many banks allow their customers to transfer money electronically between accounts, either within the same bank or to accounts at other banks. This can be done through the bank's website or mobile app.

5. Debit and credit cards: These cards allow users to make purchases or withdraw cash from ATMs using funds that are stored electronically in a bank account. When a purchase is made using a debit or credit card, the money is transferred electronically from the user's bank account to the merchant.*
